# ADR-025: Relay-Admissible Announcement Witness by Constant Elision

**Status:** Decided and adopted for the STATE generation; implementation lands with the route track `T11-101` through `T11-108` under ruling 79 at (`[PLAN-rule:phase6:wave9-rulings]`); the historical whole-item schedule remains the archived run's replay form.
**Scope:** How a canonical metadata argument crosses the target's admission policy, in every generation this repository emits for a taproot script path.
**Supersedes:** Nothing verbatim; refines ruling 70's no-route-taken clause at (`[PLAN-rule:phase6:wave9-rulings]`) for the route alone, and narrows the current form's reviewed status of consensus-admitted and relay-refused to the historical whole-item schedule. Finding 91 at (`[PLAN-sec:phase6:wave7-findings]`) records ownership of the target limit; the reviewed status itself is in `packages/target-elements/src/transaction_form.rs`.
**Does not establish:** Relayability under a positive fee floor; root freshness; Wave 12's resource closure; any artifact promotion; or the accepted run itself.

## Context · `sec:relay-witness:context`

The adopted inputs are the [relay-witness split study](../plans/drafts/relay-witness-split-study.md) and the [constant-elision proposal](../plans/drafts/relay-witness-constant-elision-proposal.md). They expose a transport mismatch: the type's canonical encoding is larger than one initial argument may be, although the executing program may reconstruct and authenticate that encoding. The proposal's Rust sketches are illustrative and freeze no API or implementation spelling.

`TAPSCRIPT_STACK_ITEM_RELAY_LIMIT` in `packages/target-elements/src/transaction_form.rs:252-283` pins 80 bytes per initial tapscript argument at admission; its literal test is in `packages/target-elements/src/tests/transaction_form_tests.rs:143-150`. The executing script and control block are not initial arguments. This is standardness, not consensus: the form review in `packages/target-elements/src/transaction_form.rs:333-397` admits the announcement under consensus and refuses its whole-item form under relay policy. The 520-byte execution-element bound is separately stated in `packages/target-elements/src/opcode.rs:2122-2131` and `packages/target-elements/src/resource.rs:236-257`; `StackItem::new` in `packages/tapscript/src/instruction.rs:51-69` enforces the reviewed literal bound. Reconstructing 86 bytes and packing them with a 32-byte root into 118 bytes therefore does not require relaxing either bound.

The canonical encoder in `packages/realization/src/state_codec.rs:132-155` has ten layout rows: the 21-byte domain at 0..21, the four-byte schema at 21..25, five eight-byte fields at 25..65, the nine-byte maturity at 65..74, the four-byte representation nonce at 74..78, and eight reserved zeros at 78..86. The domain, schema and reserved rows are constant; the other seven rows form one contiguous 53-byte region. Constants occupy 33 bytes in 0..25 and 78..86. The independent offsets and byte-order test in `packages/realization/src/tests/state_codec_tests.rs:11-20,198-234` fix those boundaries, and the strict decoder in `packages/realization/src/state_codec.rs:174-234` refuses a wrong domain, unsupported schema or nonzero reserved byte. Classification states those existing facts without changing the committed language.

The whole-item schedule has seven roles with widths 1, 4, 8, 32, 86, 1 and 64; only index 4 violates the admission bound. `witness` in `packages/tapscript/src/state_program.rs:301-354` derives the role types from fragment preconditions. The ABI derives widths, sources, consumers and positions in `packages/transaction/src/state_abi.rs:863-933` and obtains the form review in that file at lines 751-775; the loader encodes the whole predecessor item in `packages/transaction/src/state_signing.rs:935-973`. The width limit is consequently a downstream verdict rather than an input that can alter emission. `RelayAdmissibleWitnessSplitUnopened` at `packages/transaction/src/state_abi.rs:517-561` carries the resulting unresolved transport obligation.

Authentication already depends on all canonical bytes. `authenticate` in `packages/tapscript/src/state_announcement.rs:421-446` packs root and metadata beneath the predecessor prefix; `hash_metadata` at lines 391-417 streams all 86 metadata bytes into the metadata-leaf hash, combines that hash with the retained static root, hashes the tweak and returns to tweak verification. Subsequent fragments use fixed canonical offsets. Copy-through at lines 516-532 already writes the successor's maturity tag, requested cycle, witnessed nonce and eight reserved zeros in-script. The metadata leaf still commits exact canonical bytes at (`[PLAN-rule:guide14-exec:metadata-leaf]`); moving constant transport cannot shorten that commitment.

The linker already carries six pushed symbols at fifteen sites, with seven further constructor-only symbols, as the independent census in `packages/linker/src/tests/state_symbol_tests.rs:190-233` demonstrates. Resolution checks missing definitions, incompatible types and unused definitions in `packages/linker/src/state_symbol.rs:866-902`. A constant header belongs beside those linked constants: requiring it in every argument frame duplicates information the type and program already fix.

Ruling 53 at (`[PLAN-rule:phase6:wave7-rulings]`) correctly treats a witness change as a coordinated schedule, population, slicing, leaf, golden and resource change. Ruling 70 at (`[PLAN-rule:phase6:wave9-rulings]`) leaves the route unselected while continuity work proceeds. The route can now be fixed without changing that work's evidence class: metadata publication remains in the witness at (`[PLAN-rule:guide14-exec:metadata-carrier]`), the seven roles retain their explicit sources and consumers at (`[PLAN-rule:guide14-exec:witness]`), and strict transaction round trips remain required at (`[PLAN-rule:guide14-exec:canonical-bytes]`).

## Decision · `dec:relay-witness:constant-elision`

1. The witness carries only the predecessor metadata's variable region. At metadata-authentication entry the leaf reconstructs the 25-byte constant header from one linker-resolved symbol and the eight reserved zeros from one literal, using seven instructions: SWAP, PUSH header, SWAP, CAT, PUSH zeros, CAT, SWAP. Starting with root, variable, prefix, the successive stacks, deepest first, are root, prefix, variable; root, prefix, variable, header; root, prefix, header, variable; root, prefix, header-plus-variable; root, prefix, header-plus-variable, zeros; root, prefix, canonical; root, canonical, prefix. The resulting entry state is exactly the historical authentication precondition, and its existing checks then run unchanged; the metadata leaf still commits all 86 canonical bytes.
2. The frontend's only change is classifying the canonical layout rows as constant, with their values, or variable. The codec states no target limit and learns none; its encoder and strict decoder remain unchanged. Deriving transport from these rows gives encoding agreement one checkable source instead of an independent offset table.
3. The target's argument-width policy is an input to emission. The backend derives the carried contiguous variable span from the classification, combines preceding constants into one symbol and following constants into a literal, and returns a typed emission error for non-contiguous variable rows or any carried schedule exceeding the initial-item limit. A relay-refused program is never emitted for a new deployment. The linker independently re-measures every initial argument against the same policy, separately from the execution bound, so a declared width cannot bypass the admission rule and legitimate wider intermediates remain legal.
4. Constants are linked, never passed: the header is one 25-byte seventh pushed relocation symbol, `MetadataHeader`, width-checked at resolution, while the reserved zeros are a leaf literal. The header's value comes from the frontend's constant rows, and the suffix follows the successor-side synthesis already present; neither is a caller-controlled witness value.
5. The historical whole-item schedule remains constructible for exact replay of the archived refused run and is never selectable for a new deployment. Retaining the schedule in the composed record and replay recipe preserves the requests that the archive actually records; silently changing the builder would destroy that correspondence.
6. The route is its own track, allocated from the first free backlog number at landing, here `T11-100` through `T11-109`. Its accepted evidence supplies `T11-098`; Wave 9's node-free work continues unchanged because projection and comparison can be built before accepted bytes exist. This refines only ruling 70's route clause at (`[PLAN-rule:phase6:wave9-rulings]`).
7. Accepted evidence requires a newly funded predecessor under the new leaf and a fresh run into a second immutable four-file corpus through the existing executor and schema 8. The executor's fee-free environment is disclosed beside that evidence; acceptance alone promotes no artifact. The header is settled as one symbol and the reserved zeros as a literal; the fresh run proceeds as soon as its prerequisite bites are green, because a separate Wave-9 closure adds no missing input to that run.

## Consequences · `rem:relay-witness:consequences`

The seven argument widths become 1, 4, 8, 32, 53, 1 and 64. The metadata item has 27 bytes of headroom under 80; the largest initial item is the 64-byte signature, leaving 16 bytes overall. This corrects the proposal's sizes table on that point. Seven roles remain seven, comprising six public roles and one signer-held role; script and control remain outside the initial-argument comparison.

The archived transaction's 1,286-byte executing script, 1,694-byte serialization, 130-byte stripped serialization and weight 2,084 are pinned by `archived_source_projects` in `packages/vectors/src/maturity_continuity.rs:3049-3068`. The current emission contains 475 instructions: 448 semantic instructions from `packages/tapscript/src/state_announcement.rs:355-583`, twenty structural instructions from `packages/tapscript/src/state_pattern.rs:378-443`, two operator instructions from `packages/tapscript/src/state_operator.rs:155-160`, and five adapters from `packages/tapscript/src/state_program.rs:228-239`; explicit-prefix checks contribute two instructions each in `packages/tapscript/src/pattern.rs:647-655`. The prologue adds seven instructions and 40 encoded script bytes, while the argument loses 33 bytes. With the same transaction framing and control depth, estimates are therefore about 1,326 script bytes, 482 instructions, 1,701 transaction bytes and weight 2,091. These are source-derived estimates, not a new emitted measurement or native observation. The relocation census becomes seven pushed symbols at sixteen sites, adding one header site to the fifteen verified in `packages/linker/src/tests/state_symbol_tests.rs:190-233`.

Changing the announcement leaf changes its hash, static root, output keys, controls and signature messages, and can change selected nonces. All seven constructor goldens and both sides' selected nonces are recomputed with real arithmetic and independent framing, following `packages/tapscript/src/tests/state_constructor_tests.rs:1215-1263`; no digest is guessed from its apparent role. Full-descriptor continuity in `packages/linker/src/state_bundle.rs:373-409` refuses an old-tree predecessor paired with a new-tree successor, even though the semantic transition is unchanged. This preserves the shared-static-subtree relation at (`[PLAN-rule:guide14-exec:static-relation]`). Complete-tuple determinism includes the retained schedule, preserving the distinction recorded by `G14C-07` in `plans/guides/guide_fourteen_conceptual_register.md`; byte equality, root equality under assumptions and hidden-content equality remain distinct as `G14C-14` there requires.

The new schedule discharges only its own `RelayAdmissibleWitnessSplitUnopened` residual; the historical schedule's refusal stays on record. `measure_state_totals` in `packages/linker/src/state_resource.rs:101-183` currently measures script bytes, operation cost, validation budget, initial stack items and peak stack items, plus instruction count. Transaction weight, witness bytes, stack-element bytes, control-path depth and package limit are deliberately absent from those totals, as `packages/linker/src/tests/state_resource_tests.rs:133-143` asserts. The separate initial-argument comparison supplies admission evidence without inventing those totals or closing Wave 12.

The `wrong-domain-separator` and `nonzero-reserved-field` rows in `packages/vectors/src/maturity_safety.rs:1617-1643` lose their witness carrier under the new schedule while their declared boundary, ABI construction rejection, is unchanged. They retain codec-refusal and leaf-binding evidence and are annotated, never deleted; no prior script-path observation is asserted. Authentication equivalence holds over canonical states enforced by the codec. A predecessor encoding with altered domain, schema or reserved constants that the historical leaf could authenticate cannot be supplied through this reconstruction, which is the intended stricter behavior rather than equivalence over malformed encodings.

The projector already exists: its fixed width table is in `packages/vectors/src/maturity_continuity.rs:534`, its item-4 strict decode at lines 1527-1528, and its checked constructor evidence at lines 829-875. It must reconstruct through the same legalization from the retained schedule, with observed width used as a check rather than as a recipe selector. The study's historical statement that this module was absent is not the opening tree's state. The estimated review surface is about 38 existing files plus four new evidence files, covering classification, emission, linkage, ABI, replay, projection and their tests; it is not a promise that each consumer needs an edit or that the eventual diff has exactly that count.

`MaturityAcceptanceObligation` in `packages/vectors/src/maturity_native.rs:168-242` has only `Outstanding`; a separate established variant must carry schedule, accepted identity and readback. The existing importer pins four files and replays the refused corpus in `packages/vectors/src/maturity_corpus.rs:37-55,1040-1067`; those bytes and their loader retain their historical meaning while a second corpus admits the new schedule. Schema 8 already requires an accepted submission identity and mined readback and forbids accepted fields on refusal in `packages/target-elements-conformance/src/protocol.rs:229,3208-3225`. The planner checks txid, wtxid and exact submitted/readback bytes in `packages/vectors/src/maturity_native.rs:688-711`. Ruling 73 at (`[PLAN-rule:phase6:wave9-rulings]`) reserves `ConstructorContinuityObserved` for a validated comparison over those accepted bytes, so host reconstruction alone cannot close `T11-098`.

The executor sets `-minrelaytxfee=0` and `-blockmintxfee=0` in `scripts/elements-native-executor.py:2199-2200`. Its accepted branch requires mempool admission followed by mined readback at lines 4610-4633; its block fallback after policy refusal remains a relay refusal at lines 4650-4664. A fresh acceptance proves acceptance in that disclosed environment, not relayability under a positive fee floor. Neither that acceptance nor the transport change authenticates root freshness or promotes an artifact.

## Rejected alternatives · `rem:relay-witness:rejected`

**Two pieces at 78 and 8, other cuts, more pieces, or added size guards.** A split transports constants as arguments and adds a role even though the program can obtain those constants without witness input. Its minimal concatenate-and-reorder join enforces no partition: another partition with identical concatenation reaches the same authentication state. More pieces multiply schedule positions, while guards add transport checks without improving canonical authentication. The fixed canonical ranges are already supplied by `packages/realization/src/state_codec.rs`, and the existing authentication boundary in `packages/tapscript/src/state_announcement.rs` consumes the reconstructed whole.

**Shorten the codec.** Removing committed bytes changes the encoding language and strict refusals to solve a six-byte transport excess. Keeping the full encoding and omitting constants from its transport preserves the metadata-leaf commitment at (`[PLAN-rule:guide14-exec:metadata-leaf]`).

**Carry 78 and synthesize the zeros, or carry 61 and synthesize the header.** Either reconstructs only half of the constant classification: the first still carries a fixed header, while the second still carries reserved bytes that successor copy-through already freezes. Deriving both sides of the variable span from the layout removes that arbitrary asymmetry.

**Another leaf version or a relaxed node policy.** Another version is a different execution contract, not evidence that this one is admissible. Relaxing one node's loader does not make the same bytes admissible under the pinned target policy; that is the reason ruling 64 at (`[PLAN-rule:phase6:wave8-rulings]`) excludes it as a route. Width refusal occurs before execution and cannot be repaired by paying more or selecting a package topology, as `packages/target-elements/src/transaction_form.rs:333-365` explains.

**A block-layer submission subject or the annex.** A block-only subject would leave relay admission unsatisfied and require its own protocol revision; `MaturityAcceptanceRoute` in `packages/vectors/src/maturity_native.rs:156-165` distinguishes that route, and the existing schema refuses to label a relay refusal as accepted. The annex is outside the admitted witness profile at (`[PLAN-rule:guide14-exec:operator-profile]`); it supplies no established relay-admissible carrier for this argument.

**Bake the variable argument into the code.** A state-specific leaf makes the static subtree change with metadata, contradicting the exact shared subtree at (`[PLAN-rule:guide14-exec:static-relation]`) and replacing deployment-constant relocation with per-state program construction. The canonical metadata leaf already carries the state-specific commitment; duplicating it in the executing leaf breaks that separation.

## Adoption gate · `gate:relay-witness:adoption`

This record reads implemented only when:

- classification and encoding agree byte for byte, derived reconstruction round-trips strictly, and the existing decoder's domain, schema and reserved-byte refusals remain;
- lowering returns typed errors for non-contiguous variable rows and an over-wide carried span or schedule;
- the new seven-item map has 53 bytes at index 4 and every initial argument at most 80, with unchanged semantic outcomes and wrong, short and long variable items refused;
- old and new stacks are identical at the historical authentication entry after the seven-instruction prologue, and the single header symbol is width-checked at resolution;
- linking measures the initial-argument policy separately from the 520-byte execution bound, with 80 passing and 81 failing the admission comparator, script and control excluded, and absent resource dimensions still absent;
- all three historical requests replay byte-identically under the retained whole-item schedule, that schedule refuses new deployment, and cross-schedule replay refuses;
- every golden and both sides' selected nonces are recomputed with real arithmetic and independent framing, every lower rejected nonce is explained, and old/new-tree continuity refuses;
- the second immutable corpus admits the fresh run's accepted identity and exact mined readback, the old corpus still admits, and the accepted-byte continuity comparison supplies `T11-098` through `T11-108`;
- the route's measured dispositions pass its repository and documentation gates without claiming root freshness, positive-fee-floor relayability, resource closure or artifact promotion from acceptance alone.

Until those conditions hold, this record is decided and adopted but unimplemented. The backlog owns the bite sequence; this record owns the transport and emission rule.
