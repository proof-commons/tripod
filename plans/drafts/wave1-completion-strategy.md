## 2. The contract and its traces · `sec:wave1:contract`

**Fact (Exactly two clauses)** · `fact:wave1:whole-covenant`

Born first authenticates that the spent output was position 0 of its creating event. Continue first authenticates the same keyed covenant at spending-event output 0. Both apply at every consumed covenant output; there is no covenant authorization, value comparison or restriction on other inputs/outputs. Keyed membership is immutable and disjoint under stated realization assumptions; later covenant-shaped outputs are not handles and are never spendable (`[A-def:kpc-model:first-output]`), (`[A-def:kpc-model:keyed-membership]`), (`[A-prop:kpc-model:single-birth]`).

**Fact (SOURCE and MERGE classify handle creation)** · `fact:wave1:record-sets`

SOURCE consumes no handle of its key and creates a handle whose identity, paired with its key, is the singleton record. MERGE consumes one or more handles, assigns output 0 their union and introduces no record regardless of other created data. An event without a handle has no set to define. Sets and successors are derived from dependency history; there is no counter, accumulator, magnitude, depth, designated head or terminal (`[A-op:kpc-operations:source]`), (`[A-op:kpc-operations:merge]`), (`[A-def:kpc-operations:classification]`), (`[A-sec:kpc:operations]`).

**Fact (Reading and acceptance have their own boundaries)** · `fact:wave1:reading`

Unique continuation gives disjoint live-handle sets and path growth. Reconstruction requires authentic inclusion and complete retrieval, returns incomplete for missing evidence and invalid for conflicting identities or cycles, and computes exactly the dependency-derived set when its premises hold. Published reader acceptance filters that set without changing it; a fixed predicate on immutable SOURCE contents preserves accepted-set growth, while arbitrary changing policies need not. Finality is a named external assumption required only for a reading claimed final (`[A-thm:kpc-invariants:unique-successor]`), (`[A-thm:kpc-invariants:set-membership-growth]`), (`[A-sec:kpc:interface]`), (`[A-thm:kpc-interface:membership-correctness]`), (`[A-rem:kpc-interface:record-evidence]`).
