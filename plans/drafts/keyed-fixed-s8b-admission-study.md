## Fixture decision and measured sizing · `tab:s8b:fixtures`

Use the bounded indexed-LZMA approach of packages/vectors/src/keyed_final_corpus.rs, owned by keyed_fixed_corpus.rs, without a new extractor executable. Each compressed member contains compact JSON pairs of original relative path and lowercase hex of exact file bytes, sorted by path. This deliberately preserves JSON whitespace, raw transaction files and redundant subject/exchange representations for byte joins. Group whole files to a target of 8 MiB decoded; a single larger file gets its own member, still below the hard 32,000,000-byte bound. Use the existing LZMA reader with its 16 MiB memory limit, strict end condition, checked offset/length arithmetic and decoded-length-plus-one limit. Reject trailing compressed bytes, overlapping or uncovered indexed ranges, duplicate paths, paths outside the declared run/family, malformed hex and unknown files. The corpus reads names in memory; it never extracts or executes files at test time.

Retain every regular file supplied in the six blobs except each family's test.log, debug.log, node.out, unit.log and transport-result.json. The transport aggregate duplicates the per-subject and exchange files and alone occupies 72,962,092 bytes in scaling; the logs add no independent refusal evidence. Keep the original retention.sha256 files unchanged, with an explicit omission list checked against the archived manifest. Resolve the four sealed settings.json files from preparation. Keep exact CENSUS.json, freeze binaries, subject/payment transaction and parent files, subject/payment JSON, all request/response exchanges, observed provenance, handshake, timing, resource snapshots, commands and exit/seal records. Shell/Python files present in the packet remain inert byte evidence. Keep root's prepare.log as explicitly unsealed data; never use its marker as cryptographic seal evidence.

The following are measured candidate compressed sizes using LZMA-alone preset 0, not invented final fixture digests. The implementation repeats byte comparison and publishes exact sizes/digests in its census. Encodings, source snapshots and all payloads remain byte-identical even if compression changes. The total of these seven compressed payloads is 21,219,940 bytes; allocate 32 KiB for the index, a total of 21,252,708 bytes (about 20.27 MiB). This increases the checkout and each linked fixture-bearing test artifact by roughly that amount; Git pack savings are not assumed. No downloaded blob is checked in as a second copy.

## Historical freeze decoder · `sec:s8b:decoder`

Extend the existing encoder owner packages/vectors/src/keyed_fixed_manifest/encoding.rs with its inverse; keep the recipe and encoding unchanged. Input is the exact binary grammar already documented there: recipe ASCII and NUL; u32-BE source count; each u32-BE framed ASCII path and digest32; u32-BE computed count and explicit u16-BE codes; K32, A32, v-u64-BE; transcript42, pair32, framed leaf, version-u8, internal32, root32, tweak32, output-key32, parity-u8, framed program and control; schema/target u32-BE; maximum tag 0 or 1 with u64-BE only for 1; five u64-BE resource fields; four boolean bytes. Accept only 0/1 boolean/parity/tag values, the owned path alphabet, known codes in the reviewed order, exact framing and end of input. Bounds are checked before allocation: selected campaign encodings are exactly 8,853 bytes, 98 sources and 15 computed codes; legacy domain, truncation, overflow, duplicate/reordered sources/codes, unknown codes and trailing bytes refuse.

The manifest owner reconstructs a historical validated freeze only after decoding, independently reconstructing the descriptor from the retained triple and public recipe, checking the selected envelope (Some(1299), 104/140/143 bytes, zero depth/arguments, no annex/issuance/peg-in, empty nonce), schema 1 and target 2, re-encoding byte-for-byte, and authenticating the reviewed capture-time source map and identity. Use reconstruct_capture_history's existing source-byte/digest checks, populated with compiled unchanged sources and the two retained pre-change sources; extend the validated history to expose its authenticated complete freeze for bind_journal. Current validate continues to reject historical inventories. A caller-supplied digest matching an arbitrary encoding alone must never mint a validated freeze: the corpus's independently reviewed per-triple identities and history mapping are the trust anchor, outside the offered packet. WrongKey's foreign descriptor is derived separately and is not promoted to a captured freeze.

The selected decoder test first accepts all four campaign encodings and round-trips them exactly, then changes one encoded source-digest byte against the unchanged reviewed anchor and gets Inventory. Separate small table entries change framing, domain, boolean, computed code, triple, descriptor and envelope fields and assert their respective parsing/validation boundary beside the original accepted input. Test an authentic historical encoding after the paired current re-pin: current validation refuses it while historical replay and journal admission accept it. These are one focused decoder test, not an open-ended fuzz battery. Earlier pilot/pass encodings are outside this selected closed admission set; their source history tests remain, and the decoder design does not relabel their packets.

## Receipt limits, exclusions and questions · `tab:s8b:limits`

| Receipt limit | S8b disposition |
|---|---|
| Executable provenance is an in-container assertion | Narrow: independently join preparation/build seal/lock and all recorded provenance; no retained executable in hub, no proof of which bytes executed, no replacement binary run |
| Seven equality sites OPEN | Discharge only the sites successfully attributed by the paired bounded-model replay; otherwise retain individual OPEN sites without losing the generic RPC refusal |
| Reader results and admission traces absent | Discharge reproducibility by six fresh readings, missing-branch replay and per-row offline admission; original output/trace absence stays historical |
| RPC answers rather than rejection logs | Unchanged; bind RPC reason to submitted txid, do not claim independent node-log or block refusal proof |
| Scaling misses fourfold capture headroom | Unchanged: 985.75 s transport / 2040 s and about 1129 s unit / 3600 s fail that condition; future capture must split or re-derive on a loaded run, never raise a guard here |

Campaign3 alone enters the corpus. Pilot and both earlier passes remain separately retained outside it, including their successful families and stopped packets, at their own identities. This avoids duplicate observations and absent timing/count assumptions while preserving the record of the stops. Historical decoder controls exercise campaign3 after S8b's current re-pin, so excluding earlier runs does not leave historical admission untested. Earlier sealed families are not called invalid and are not renamed as campaign3. Sponsor/confidential/present-nonce and non-policy-asset qualification, raised-rate sessions, legacy-copy and contention remain deferred under rulings 216/218/220; winner/retry cannot be rebuilt from packets whose adapter mined before returning.

No new ruling is necessary to choose the compact representation, decoder, additional offline test target or corpus-only joins: they extend fixed-form owners under the existing packet-correction rule. Two questions remain conditional, with recommendations. If the bounded model cannot uniquely attribute a subject, should another native capture or model extension be required now? Recommend no: retain OPEN with exact failed premise and let S9/ruling authority decide further evidence; this follows the existing non-claim rule. If future capture must satisfy ruling 215's fourfold condition, should scaling split or its bound change? Recommend split from measured loaded-run bands; S8b cannot decide a new capture budget or waive the recorded failure. Neither question blocks the selected offline admission or silently tightens an existing rule. A needed contract change outside the fixed-form track stops the implementation for renewed adoption.
