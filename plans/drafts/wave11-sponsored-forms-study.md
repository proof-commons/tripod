# Study: Wave 11's sponsored maturity forms against the tree and their bites

Status: a design study over the tree at `0.6.268-dev`, the Wave-10 closure record at (`sec:phase6:wave10-findings`) with the first bite of `plans/drafts/wave10-register-closure-study.md` landed over it, written because Wave 11 at (`task:guide14-exec:wave11`) asks for nine deliverables and three exits over sponsored announcement forms while the tree builds no sponsored STATE candidate: construction refuses both sponsored forms by `SponsoredMaturityFormHasNoCarrier` under ruling 58 at (`rule:phase6:wave7-rulings`), the conceptual register gives the wave no owned row (`plans/guides/guide_fourteen_conceptual_register.md:154`), and a bite cannot be cut against a deliverable whose subject the tree declines to build. Every figure below is recounted from the source at `0.6.268-dev` or read from the assertion that pins it; nothing here was obtained by running a test, and no line of production code is changed by this file. It answers eight design questions as decisions D1 to D8, leaves three questions with both sides, proposes seven rulings R1 to R7 for an opening record, manufactures four code bites with their exact shapes, and states three conditional bites it does not cut.

## 0. In one breath

The request type already admits every form the wave names — the sponsored and sponsorless forms crossed with the sponsor-change choice — and the tree already accounts for three of the four: the sponsorless form without change is accepted by a real target in the admitted archive, the sponsorless form with change is refused by the request's own constructor, and the sponsored form without change is refused at construction by a refusal that names the carrier it lacks; the fourth, sponsored with change, reaches the same refusal on the same line and no test pins it. What the tree does not have is any sponsored byte: under ruling 40 the reduced leaf carries no sponsor check, under ruling 52 the five whole-transaction relations wait on the region-scoping refit `T11-064`, and ruling 58 declines to emit a form no leaf constrains. The study finds that objection narrower than its wording — the realization already evaluates a single-operation sponsored announcement and refuses sponsor faults on it, as ADR-024 says the model does until the refit lands — but no weaker where it matters: the sponsored form's defining negatives, membership and multiplicity, stand on exactly the relations the refit re-scopes or retires, and a sponsored positive accepted before they have a boundary would stand without the refusals that make an acceptance mean anything. So the wave opens now, as the wave that accounts for every form by acceptance, refusal or explicit unsupported status: one bite pins the fourth form, one shows at the model that sponsorship moves no announcement relation outside the sponsor family, one pins that no canonical report publishes a sponsor key, and one retypes the matrix so that the two sponsored positives and the twelve sponsor rows stand on reasons that say what each waits for. The sponsored builder, a sponsored native ceremony and every node-bound deliverable stay conditional on `T11-064`, which remains the first bite of Phase 7's composition work, and on a compute node carrying an Elements toolchain, which the nodes in service lack.

## 1. The problem, exactly

Wave 11 names nine deliverables — "sponsorless control; sponsored control; sponsor change absent; sponsor change present where supported; missing sponsor authorization; STATE/sponsor overlap; balanced STATE-corruption attempt; sponsor-report exclusion tests; exact target and policy verdicts" — and three exits: "sponsorship changes no STATE semantic result; no sponsor amount enters protocol evidence; every sponsor form is accounted for by acceptance, refusal, or explicit unsupported status" (`plans/guides/guide_fourteen.md:3149–3172`). The register's wave table gives it no owned row, because "sponsorship consumes the Wave-7 ABI and shared observation vocabulary" (`plans/guides/guide_fourteen_conceptual_register.md:154`), and the Phase-6 gate reads the wave through its eighth conjunct, "every claimed sponsored form has an explicit disposition" (`plans/guides/guide_fourteen.md:3596`).

The guide's sponsor contract is §1.13 (`rule:guide14-exec:sponsor-opacity`): no semantic relation, compiler plan, target program, canonical report or future identity requires an individual sponsor amount to be decoded for protocol use, explicit for protocol use, opened, compared with zero, proved positive, included in a public subtotal, emitted in a canonical report or emitted in ordinary diagnostics; sponsor safety derives from "exact sponsor-region membership", "STATE/sponsor disjointness", "sponsor-owner target authorization", "at most one sponsor envelope", "whole-transaction target conservation" and "independent STATE-succession enforcement"; and "A balanced corruption that adjusts sponsor change while modifying STATE must still fail for the STATE relation" (`plans/guides/guide_fourteen.md:381–412`). §7.2 fixes input closure as "STATE plus optional sponsor only", output closure as "STATE successor plus optional sponsor change and fee only" and sponsor isolation as "exact, disjoint, amount-opaque" (`plans/guides/guide_fourteen.md:1200–1202`); §10.9's fragment "reads no sponsor value field" and authenticates the suffix, the reserve asset, the change program and role, the fee program and role, disjointness and counts (`plans/guides/guide_fourteen.md:1711–1722`); and §16.10 requires fourteen sponsor faults (`plans/guides/guide_fourteen.md:2620–2637`).

The tree removed the STATE leaf's half of that contract deliberately. Ruling 40 strips the exact counts, the sponsor-suffix sweep, the change and fee checks and the shape literal from the announcement leaf as surplus to closure, supersedes §10.2's sponsor clauses and the `StateSponsorIsolationV1` census entry for the STATE generation, and keeps input zero's asset, explicit amount and script version and output zero's asset, explicit amount and authenticated successor program (`plans/phases/06-state-and-maturity.md:228`, `plans/guides/guide_fourteen_conceptual_register.md:99`). Ruling 52 classes the five relations the realization evaluates over the whole observed transaction — `AllowedObjectFamilies`, `SponsorIsolation`, `CanonicalDeltaPolicy`, `OpenFlowPolicy` and `SponsorEnvelopeMultiplicity` — MODEL-SCOPE until the region-scoping refit of `T11-064` gives them a region (`plans/phases/06-state-and-maturity.md:240`, `packages/realization/src/evaluate.rs:414–494`, `packages/realization/src/relation.rs:56–95`). Ruling 58 therefore builds only the sponsorless candidate and refuses the sponsored arm by name (`plans/phases/06-state-and-maturity.md:251`), which is why Wave 7's second exit stands CONDITIONALLY MET on `SponsoredMaturityFormHasNoCarrier` (`plans/phases/06-state-and-maturity.md:686`) and why the half of `G14C-07` over distinct admitted sponsor environments did not close (`plans/phases/06-state-and-maturity.md:692`).

Every deliverable but the first and the eighth therefore presumes a sponsored candidate the tree declines to build. The question the wave opens on is not how to exercise sponsored bytes but whether the wave can be accounted for without them, and, where it cannot, exactly what it waits on and who owns that.
