Its cell leaves the relation, the general theorem and supported-step completeness with Waves 2 and 13 (`plans/guides/guide_fourteen_conceptual_register.md:110`).

`G14C-15` asks that "Wave 13's result matrix and handoff state that only maturity announcement is compiled, later STATE operations and constructor migration remain unavailable, the successor may be intentionally inert, and no release-complete state-machine or lifecycle-wide coinduction claim is made; an audit test or plans-validator rule fails if the Phase-6 gate record promotes the candidate to final or omits those non-claims" (`plans/guides/guide_fourteen_conceptual_register.md:123`). ## 3. Decisions

**D1. The gate record reads §23 through one table of twenty-three rows plus the register conjunct. Each row carries its standing, its evidence by tag, test name or record label, and any condition with what carries it. §23 reads PASSED only when every row reads MET. At this base it reads NOT PASSED: fifteen MET, five CONDITIONALLY MET and three OUTSTANDING, with the register conjunct OUTSTANDING. After every bite this study cuts, it still reads NOT PASSED, on conjuncts 5, 7, 11, 13 and 14.**

§23 is a conjunction, and "A typed stopped result is valid and does not satisfy this exit gate" rules out reading a recorded limitation as a pass (`plans/guides/guide_fourteen.md:3613`). A row that states a condition asserts less than its conjunct, so the conjunction over such rows asserts less than §23. The arithmetic belongs to the gate and is done in the record. The exit decision belongs to the owner-reviewed closeout, which takes it over that arithmetic rather than in its place.

The table is the only form in which each condition stays visible; a summary sentence would carry the count and lose the carriers. A row with no citation is OUTSTANDING by construction, because an unsupported MET is the stronger claim Wave 13's second exit forbids. Conjuncts 22 and 23 are verdicts of runs, so they stay OUTSTANDING until the record cites the run.

No host bite can reach the five conditional conjuncts:

- conjunct 5 needs native refusals at the script-path and key-path boundaries;

### Bite 3 — the abstraction relation, forward simulation over the accepted step, and supported-step completeness

Files:

- `packages/vectors/src/maturity_refinement.rs` (new);
- its `pub mod maturity_refinement;` line in `packages/vectors/src/lib.rs`;

Shape (D3): the typed abstraction relation; the theorem's clauses as a closed vocabulary with their witnesses; the forward check over accepted steps; the finite completeness check; and the non-claims.

Types:

- `MaturityBoundEnvironment`: lead bounds, static subtree, internal key, reviewed target contract and deployment identity, read from a retained linked bundle, with the branch context carried as caller-stated.
- `MaturityConcreteState`: one STATE output's program, with its witnessed encoded metadata.
- `abstract_state(concrete, environment) -> Result<StateMetadata, MaturityAbstractionRefusal>`: the relation as a function.
  - It decodes the witnessed metadata canonically and reconstructs the output program at the witnessed nonce under the environment.
  - Only when the reconstruction equals the program does it return the semantic projection, which erases the nonce.
  - Its refusal has the members `MetadataDoesNotDecode`, `ReconstructionRefused` and `ProgramDiffers`.
- `MaturityStepStanding`: `AcceptedWithTargetIdentity` or `HostProjectedOnly`.
