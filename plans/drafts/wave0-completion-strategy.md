# Research: completing Wave 0 of the Guide 15 execution guide

**Abstract (Proposal status and execution scope)** · `abst:wave0:scope`

This proposal studies the order, evidence, acceptance rulings and measurements needed to complete inherited debt and preflight before new operations begin. It binds nothing, changes no rule and accepts no position; the seven bites and their common exit remain governed by the execution guide. Proposed work is not completed evidence, and the research note defines no production policy (`task:guide15-exec:wave0`), (`gate:guide15-exec:wave0`), (`cav:guide15-exec:planned-evidence`), (`rule:research:status`).

**Definition (A closure evidence packet)** · `def:wave0:closure-packet`

A closure evidence packet is the proposed unit joining a source obligation, exact subject, prerequisite ruling, performed check, retained artifact, measured result and closing record. It lets a later review follow the result to its subject without treating status as evidence.

**Definition (A proposed execution step)** · `def:wave0:execution-step`

A proposed execution step is one bounded change or observation with a declared input and a predicate that determines whether the next step may begin. Its name includes its bite and a descriptive slug; size measures touched boundaries and rows rather than elapsed time.

**Argument (Backward planning from the exit)** · `arg:wave0:backward-planning`

Begin with missing packet fields, not broad package work. A row lacking a constructible mutant opens a staging step; a row with bytes but no accepted control opens a control step; a row with observations but no validated consumer opens an admission step. This makes the distinction required by (`rule:guide15-exec:finding-boundary`) operational.

**Criterion (Evidence sufficient to open the next step)** · `crit:wave0:step-readiness`

A step is ready only when every required input names a retained artifact or accepted ruling and its stopping predicate can be evaluated without inventing a policy. A downstream step may inspect incomplete inputs, but cannot claim their acceptance or publish a closure packet from them.

**Fact (Model law and later declarations are separate)** · `fact:wave0:semantic-split`

Wave 0 identifies model law and field ownership while missing burn and clear declarations remain later implementation work. The two DOES NOT HOLD rows therefore receive explicit later proof obligations rather than fabricated passes (`def:guide15-exec:semantic-entry-split`), (`cond:guide15:operation-scope`), (`cond:guide15:validated-relations`).

**Fact (Numbered acceptance is distinct from a gate result)** · `fact:wave0:acceptance-boundary`

The seven assigned dispositions and nine position-bearing entries need numbered Phase-7 acceptance. A successful test answers its observation question but cannot accept a generation policy, carrier selection or signing profile (`req:guide15-exec:position-acceptance`), (`crit:guide15-exec:position-versus-observation`).

**Argument (Observable failure leaves an obligation open)** · `arg:wave0:observable-failure`

Plan an explicit stop at every native acceptance predicate. If the pinned target refuses the intended positive form, retain the failed subject and diagnose its cause; do not proceed by deleting the only observable form. Any replacement must still perform the retained obligation (`rule:guide15-exec:observable-retention`).

**Caveat (Final reasons cannot rename waiting work)** · `cav:wave0:finality-limit`

A final reason proves why the claim needs no further observation within its admitted meaning. Missing bytes, a missing control or missing provisioning are unfinished work and cannot become a final reason merely because they are difficult (`def:guide15-exec:non-answer-finality`), (`def:guide15-exec:retired-residual`).

**Strategy (Close evidence chains before consolidating the baseline)** · `strat:wave0:evidence-first`

Settle the exact subject, demonstrate construction, validate the positive control, exercise the fault or independent observation, admit it through its typed consumer, and only then close its carrier. This order exposes cheaper construction failures before expensive observations and preserves the guide's prerequisite graph (`alg:guide15-exec:wave0-graph`).

**Proposal (Carry one evidence packet per closing subject)** · `proposal:wave0:closure-packets`

Accept packets by subject even when a single run serves several subjects. Require an explicit equality argument before sharing an observation, and retain separate verdicts for Safety, relation coverage, continuity and recovery. This makes reuse reviewable without multiplying executions unnecessarily (`rule:guide15-exec:inherited-deliverables`).

**Caveat (Proposed steps establish no completed observation)** · `cav:wave0:proposal-evidence`

All future files, evidence directories, checks and closure records below are instructions proposed for a later bite. Their presence in this note does not establish native evidence, production readiness or a completed Wave-0 entry (`cav:guide15-exec:planned-evidence`), (`cav:guide15-exec:production-non-claims`).
