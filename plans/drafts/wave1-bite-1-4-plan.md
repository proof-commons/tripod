# Wave 1 bite-1.4 landing plan · `sec:w1b14:plan`

The initial study below proposed a sequence and decisions; (`rec:w1b14:adoption`) now governs them. It read `0.7.108-dev`, takes the explicit handoff from (`rec:w1b13:closed`) and (`rec:w1b13:members`), and implements (`sec:wave1:bite-1-4`), (`proposal:wave1:bite-1-4`), (`task:guide15-exec:bite-1-4-deliverables`) and (`setup:guide15-exec:bite-1-4-files-and-packages`). Rulings 162 and 163 at (`rule:phase7:wave1-realization-rulings`) and 180 at (`rule:phase7:wave1-home-rulings`) govern the construction envelope. No build, test or native execution was run for this study; the documentation gate checks this plan, not its implementation.

**Disposition (Adopted sequence and decisions)** · `rec:w1b14:adoption`

The six-step sequence is adopted for bite 1.4. The four recommendations below are adopted with their reasons and review boundaries; the original proposals remain as the record of the reasoning.

(`q:w1b14:curve`) is adopted: production curve arithmetic is a transaction-owned adapter over the existing workspace `secp256k1-zkp` safe wrapper, with transaction's existing tagged hash computing the Elements tweak. This adds no second curve or hash implementation, keeps the independent conformance arithmetic as the comparison oracle, and places curve provenance with the commitment owner. Step 2 records the dependency-role change from reference oracle to production in the transaction contract and root dependency documentation, including the exact safe API, features and exceptional cases. If the wrapper cannot implement the fixed recipe, step 2 stops and names the missing capability.

(`q:w1b14:inputs`) is adopted: public requests take parent transaction bytes, selected outpoints and checked spending capabilities; content addressing and canonical decoding are validated locally. The first packet supports only the existing decoder's envelope, with no issuance or peg-in anywhere in a parent. Unsupported serialization produces a typed unsupported-form result, not a covenant-invalid parent. Inclusion, unspentness, retrieval and finality remain external obligations, because local reproduction is not chain recognition and widening the shared decoder needs its own serialization review.

(`q:w1b14:envelope`) is adopted: typed structural construction is separate from admission against the measured policy envelope. Only the recorded envelope — one P2WPKH sponsor, successor/change/fee outputs, explicit policy asset and the sponsor-signature bound — earns the 1,564 maximum; a request outside it cannot present as admitted. Step 4 proposes the two exact entry and error shapes for review. Rulings 162 and 163 admit a measured envelope, not a general law.

(`q:w1b14:surface`) is adopted: linker owns `LinkedKeyedBundle`; transaction owns distinct SOURCE and MERGE request types with private checked fields, sealed authenticated input and commitment values, a separate ordinary funding-signature phase and immutable materialized output. No revalidating entry is added in this bite. Exact names, projections and errors are reviewed after steps 1 and 3, before step 4 depends on them. The zero-relocation reading is approved in step 1 only once its occurrence inventory is checkable. This keeps one authority boundary per artifact and preserves owner errors intact.

Independent review follows steps 1, 2, 3 and 4, and examines the gate packet at step 6; step 5 is reviewed with that packet. These checkpoints settle reference closure, commitment provenance, authenticated classification and construction admission before their downstream consumers rely on them.

## Ownership and starting boundary · `sec:w1b14:boundary`

Production already has the immutable selected emission bundle, complete `KeyedLeafRecipe`, resource formulas, target assessment and supplied-context member receipt. It does not yet authenticate predecessors or link/materialize keyed transactions. Preserve the distinction between `OfflinePredecessorAssertion` and authenticated production inputs: the former is an oracle input, never a receipt proving the latter (`packages/tapscript/src/keyed_members.rs:43`). SOURCE's four and MERGE's three evidence obligations survive construction; a content-addressed previous transaction proves neither its inclusion nor its history classification.

## Six reviewable steps · `sec:w1b14:steps`

### 1. Close the keyed link packet · `step:w1b14:link`

**Scope: 8–12 files.** Proposed `packages/linker/src/keyed_bundle.rs`, keyed reference/parameter module and tests, with `lib.rs`, Meson, README/TRACING and `plans/packages/linker.md`. Consume the sealed emission bundle and reviewed target; retain binding, scope, selected proof/placement identity, relation coverage, evidence and construction material. Publish an immutable `LinkedKeyedBundle`, not caller-editable script bytes. Reuse byte encoding, recipe and resource kernels; leave compact/live/STATE linkers unchanged.

**Closure.** Inventory constructor fields, instruction literals and authenticated introspection references separately. The key is already embedded by emission and the successor comparison is current-program introspection: do not invent a hash placeholder, relocate the semantic key again or seek a self-hash fixed point. Propose a zero mandatory byte-relocation census for the current fixed leaf, verified against its typed material, with every constructor field resolved once and every introspection reference retained. If inspection reveals a genuine symbolic site, declare its typed occurrence and resolve it once in the same pass; do not broaden the program or silently claim zero. A caller-supplied current/successor program is not a link parameter.

**Controls/gate/handoff.** Two keys, all three scopes and permuted parameter offers produce deterministic matching bytes and exact closure. Refuse missing/duplicate/unknown constructor bindings, target/recipe mismatch, attempted introspection substitution and unresolved references beside controls; internal synthetic census mutants test extra or multiply-resolved sites without inventing reachable public defects. Gate linker plus the common checks. Discharges the deterministic bundle and single-resolution clauses of (`task:guide15-exec:bite-1-4-deliverables`); hands closed material to step 2. Review this reference inventory before adding commitment.

### 2. Authenticate the public commitment recipe · `step:w1b14:commitment`

**Scope: 10–16 files.** Proposed `packages/transaction/src/keyed_curve.rs`, `keyed_taproot.rs` and tests; transaction manifest/exports/census/docs, root dependency-role documentation and vectors consumer tests. Subject to (`q:w1b14:curve`), implement the existing supplied curve capability with the existing safe wrapper; reuse transaction hashing and tapscript material. Build the sole reviewed leaf, root, output x/parity, witness program and depth-zero control block. Keep recipe schema, NUMS derivation, tagged domains, big-endian tweak scalar and point-sum convention exact; no new curve or hash implementation.

**Controls/gate/handoff.** Fresh public reconstruction of two keys equals the unchanged independent descriptor oracle byte for byte. Refuse non-liftable internal key, overflow/infinity where representable by a capability test, wrong leaf/version/parity/root/control, and another key's otherwise valid recipe substituted for a predecessor. Distinguish constructor-policy refusal, commitment authentication and execution; keep every accepted twin. Inventory exactly one script path containing both clauses and the unavoidable key path, conditional on the recorded NUMS/discrete-log, preimage, second-preimage and collision assumptions. Gate transaction, linker, vectors and conformance plus common checks. Discharges authenticated control/reference closure; hands a sealed commitment and production authentication function to step 3. Independent review is valuable here, especially dependency provenance and control-byte conventions.

### 3. Derive the serialized input census and typed requests · `step:w1b14:inputs`

**Scope: 12–18 files.** Proposed transaction `keyed_inputs.rs`, `keyed_request.rs`, `keyed_abi.rs` and tests, registrations/contracts and public-consumer vectors. Decode offered predecessor bytes with the existing decoder, recompute their transaction identifiers with existing SHA-256 machinery over stripped serialization, check each selected outpoint and output, and authenticate its script against the linked recipe before deriving roles. Preserve actual transaction indices and reject duplicate outpoints. Claimed counts, roles and scripts are offers to check, not authority. The exact supported predecessor envelope is decided at (`q:w1b14:inputs`).

**Classification.** A typed SOURCE request consumes no handle of its key and produces its handle at output 0; a typed MERGE consumes at least one authenticated handle of its key and instantiates both clauses on every such input. Detect a same-program keyed input offered as an ordinary sponsor before excluding sponsors by role. A later-position same-program spend cannot be hidden as collateral to bypass born-first. Other inputs are retained only with their own checked spending/funding capability; this is not an input-0 restriction or a blanket ban on unrelated target-valid spends. No caller-supplied record total substitutes for the derived census. Transaction derives the context consumed by the unchanged member binder; its sealed authentication result is distinct from the public offline assertion.

**Controls/gate/handoff.** SOURCE with zero handles and multi-input MERGE at nonzero indices succeed; SOURCE with a concealed same-key member, empty MERGE, mismatched parent hash/vout/script, wrong-key association, later-born member, duplicate input, stale index, false sponsor role and flagged member refuse at their declared authentication/request boundaries. A consistent input permutation preserves the relation-to-member map; a single wrong member fails beside an unchanged passing member. Preserve collateral sequence/flag facts without adding a leaf predicate. Gate transaction/linker/vectors plus common checks. Discharges authenticated classification and lower-bound clauses; hands checked requests and a receipt to step 4. Review classification and sponsor-role completeness before funding/materialization.

### 4. Materialize and admit the construction envelope · `step:w1b14:construct`

**Scope: 12–18 files.** Proposed transaction `keyed_construct.rs`, keyed policy/funding module and tests, existing sponsor/bytes adapters only where necessary, ABI/exports/census/contracts and vector consumers. Reuse the checked request, linked commitment, existing sponsor funding/signing capability, target form facts and keyed resource formulas. Assemble output 0 from sealed material, preserve ordered permitted collateral inputs/outputs, and attach the keyed leaf/control witness with no execution arguments or covenant signature. Funding signing requests and externally supplied ordinary signatures remain a separate phase; no creator secret is an input to keyed construction. Freeze non-witness bytes before external signing and verify returned signatures/witness budgets without changing the selected plan.

**Policy and controls.** Separate consensus balance/form checks, explicit policy readings and interface/evidence obligations. Positive explicit policy-asset handles and an explicit fee are construction choices under ruling 162; no confidential-output support, amount equality or asset equality enters the leaf. Derive fees from the actual serialization and declared sponsor witness bound; check every output for applicable relay policy. Neither the historic 27/34-satoshi anchors nor the 10–100-satoshi bracket is a universal funding floor. Exercise fee shortage, imbalance, omitted fee, wrong output-0 position/program, caller script injection, counterfeit totals and witness-budget overflow beside controls. Preserve ordinary extra outputs and target-valid redistribution under a compatible admitted envelope; unsupported envelopes get a construction-policy result, not a covenant refusal (`q:w1b14:envelope`).

Authenticate/classify the census before applying ruling 180's exact `KeyedConstructionError::HandleCountExceedsPolicy { offered, maximum }`. Under the recorded one-sponsor/three-output explicit envelope and at-most-72-byte sponsor signature, test 1,564 accepted and 1,565 refused before transaction serialization, using `1085 + 255*N` at the large-N boundary and exact CompactSize formulas elsewhere. Cross-check whole-transaction `weight()` and the 91-byte keyed input witness (88-byte script stack plus three empty-field prefixes); do not silently set deployment/package or non-covenant resource terms to zero. This is a construction-policy maximum, never a script or declaration law. Gate transaction/linker/vectors plus common checks. Hands deterministic typed artifacts and explicit outstanding obligations to step 5; independent review should examine funding finalization and the envelope, not infer policy from leaf execution.

Discharges public fresh-process reproduction and the negative-boundary clauses of (`gate:wave1:bite-1-4-packet`) and (`gate:guide15-exec:bite-1-4-gate`). ## Decisions needed before implementation · `sec:w1b14:questions`

**Production curve admission** · `q:w1b14:curve`

Recommend a transaction-owned adapter over the existing workspace `secp256k1-zkp` safe wrapper, with transaction's existing tagged hash computing the Elements tweak and the wrapper performing lift/tweak/point arithmetic. `Cargo.toml:49` currently admits this dependency as a reference oracle for tests, and `packages/transaction/Cargo.toml` has no curve dependency: production use requires an explicit contract/dependency-role disposition before step 2. Do not move or copy the conformance BigInt implementation, add raw FFI, or claim independence from the node's underlying library. Keep the independent oracle as the comparison. Review the exact safe API, exceptional cases and dependency features in step 2; if it cannot implement the fixed recipe without a second implementation, stop with the unsupported capability identified.

**Authenticated input boundary** · `q:w1b14:inputs`

Recommend parent transaction bytes plus selected outpoints and checked spending capabilities as the public request input; validate content addressing and canonical decoding locally, while retaining inclusion, unspentness, retrieval and finality as external obligations. Do not trust a claimed txid/script/record count or treat local reproduction as chain recognition. The present decoder refuses issuance and peg-in anywhere in a parent (`packages/transaction/src/bytes.rs:710`), so the initial supported predecessor envelope must say so, including collateral flagged inputs in a parent; unsupported serialization is not a covenant-invalid parent. Recommend this explicit subset for the first packet rather than expanding the shared decoder. Any broader predecessor support needs a separately scoped serialization review. At the spending-input boundary refuse flagged members and preserve ordinary target-valid collateral through checked capabilities, not through a new ASH restriction.

**Open construction versus measured policy envelope** · `q:w1b14:envelope`

Recommend separating the typed structural construction result from policy admission. Structural construction preserves permitted target-valid extra inputs and outputs; the measured standard envelope additionally requires exactly the recorded one P2WPKH sponsor, successor/change/fee outputs, explicit policy asset and sponsor signature bound. Only that envelope earns the 1,564 maximum; a changed output count, sponsor type, asset/witness form or standard-weight policy must not inherit it. Such a request can retain a structural artifact with an explicit unadmitted-policy result, or be refused by the admitted-construction entry, but cannot masquerade as admitted. Step 4 should propose the exact two entry/error shapes for review. Policy readings and fee selection are explicit request data checked against computed bytes; final admission under ruling 162 still requires the admitted measurement-(e) observations repeated on final bytes, which bite 1.6 owns. This avoids choosing a new dust threshold or claiming the historic measurement is the final funding recipe.

**Stable request/linked surface** · `q:w1b14:surface`

Recommend linker-owned `LinkedKeyedBundle` and transaction-owned distinct SOURCE/MERGE request types with private checked fields, sealed authenticated input/commitment values, a separate ordinary funding-signature phase and immutable materialized output. No caller-authored covenant script, record total, creator secret or vectors descriptor enters the production authority boundary. Exact public names/projections/errors should be reviewed after steps 1 and 3, before step 4 depends on them; retain owner errors without collapsing their stage or identity. The current leaf has no unresolved literal: approve the zero-relocation reading in step 1 only after its occurrence inventory is checkable. No second planner or automatic candidate selection is proposed.

## Keyed link closure for review · `rec:w1b14:link`

Step 1 adds `packages/linker/src/keyed_bundle.rs` and `keyed_reference.rs` under (`rule:linker:keyed-closure`). `link_keyed_bundle` consumes only the sealed `KeyedEmissionBundle`, the uniquely reviewed target and typed offers. It retains the entire emission publication by value: binding, scope, selected proof and placement identity, relation/coverage map, unresolved evidence, fragments and construction material. It neither invokes compiler planning nor authenticates a predecessor, performs a curve operation or materializes a transaction. Existing compact/live/STATE linkers, compiler plans, tapscript constructors, resource kernels and all prior expected values are unchanged. No production dependency is added.

The review disposition narrows the original seven-constructor-field inventory to two offers: an independently built recipe equality assertion and a target-version check. Fixed constants echoed by callers assert nothing and no later step reads them; echoing emission's own recipe likewise asserts nothing. Step 3 builds its recipe offer from its request key, never from emission. Schema 1 of `KeyedLeafRecipe` in `packages/tapscript/src/keyed_constructor.rs` is the single normative statement of internal-key derivation, sole-leaf tree, leaf-hash serialization, tagged domains, big-endian tweak without reduction (zero valid; overflow and infinity refuse), Q = P + tG and Q's parity, control layout and witness-v1 output form. Step 2 implements and authenticates that recipe, not linker constants. The former Tree/HashDomains/Control/InternalKey and leaf-version offers are removed; finding 8's same-type mutations of removed fields therefore no longer apply.

The dead material-version and constant-sibling checks are removed. Restating the grammar independently is intentional fail-closed coupling, not a second emitter.

The public surface is two modules, eleven types and two free functions. `keyed_bundle` exposes immutable `LinkedKeyedBundle` (`emission`, `project`, `closure`, `bytes`), non-exhaustive `KeyedLinkRefusal` (seven variants, `refusal_kind`) and sole producer `link_keyed_bundle`.
