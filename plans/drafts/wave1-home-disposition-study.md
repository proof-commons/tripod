**Judgment (Ruling 158)** · `proposal:w1home:boundary-verdict`

Recommend accepting the home on this evidence, subject to the governing disposition; this draft cannot accept it. This is not a claim that stable declarations or production keyed analysis already exist. ## 3. Proposed rulings for disposition · `sec:w1home:rulings`

The five-section study packet was adopted as rulings 171–180 at (`rule:phase7:wave1-home-rulings`), which now govern; ruling 175 removes declaration-side features and updates their workspace consumers instead of retaining compatibility aliases. The proposals below are retained as the record of the reasoning, including that superseded alias proposal. Each implementation acceptance condition belongs to the stated later packet, not to this documents-only study.

**Proposal (171 — Semantic home)** · `proposal:w1home:home`

171. Accept the explicit Keyed Provenance semantic profile in existing architecture and realization, with the shared compiler engine, as the bite-1.1 home. Reason: the standalone census/binding and scoped identifiers isolate the contracts, and the engine refit removes the first candidate's one refuting dispatch without weakening retained first-contract results. Evidence: (`proposal:w1home:boundary-verdict`), (`tab:w1home:options`), `packages/architecture/src/validate.rs:247`, `packages/compiler/src/engine_program.rs:147`. Acceptance of stable declarations still requires the packet below and (`gate:guide15-exec:bite-1-1-gate`); accepting the home does not accept a realization or close bite 1.2. Reopen at the candidate audit on a new identity-selected policy decision outside the three owner entries, leaked attestation law, changed first-contract projection/refusal boundary, or a justified independent ownership/dependency requirement for another crate.

**Proposal (172 — Opaque engine keys and adapter scope)** · `proposal:w1home:keys`

172. Keep engine records crate-private and adopt complete namespace-qualified opaque identity keys for identity-only positions, rather than extending a closed union with each owner's vocabulary; schedule this internal reduction with bite 1.2's production keyed entry, not as a new stable declaration format. Reason: `OperationKey`/`RelationKey` already demonstrate equality/order without policy (`packages/compiler/src/engine_records.rs:175`), whereas `CaseKey`, `CarrierKey`, `LayoutKey`, `ExpressionKey` and coverage graph keys still embed owner types (`packages/compiler/src/engine_placement.rs:15`, `packages/compiler/src/engine_placement.rs:30`, `packages/compiler/src/engine_foundation.rs:9`, `packages/compiler/src/engine_coverage_graph.rs:18`). Opaque means a typed key with an injective, namespace-qualified complete owner identity and owner-held reverse registry, not a hash, traversal index, pointer or caller-supplied policy flag; keys expose equality/order, no semantic callbacks. Keep semantic evidence/discharge variants explicit: they are obligations, not opaque identities. Acceptance: enumerate each replaced identity position and registry, retain exact projections and typed refusal payloads, prove same-local-code cross-owner separation, and recount exposed adapter types/functions and conversion arms before/after; promise no numerical reduction in advance and keep conversions still required for the legacy view. Retained original sources and fixed-owner validation must remain outside any erased identity shortcut. Reopen at the candidate audit on information loss, key collisions, engine-to-owner policy callbacks, worse typed refusals, or conversion complexity without an auditable reduction; use ruling 170's B′/A comparison only after an actual B stop, with thresholds fixed beforehand.

**Proposal (173 — Stable manifest identity)** · `proposal:w1home:binding`

173. Replace `KeyedManifest::probe()` with proposed `KeyedManifest::contract()` and give the keyed manifest its own versioned identity, independent of attestation `DOCUMENT`, deployment and the particular generated covenant key. Reason: current domain `tripod keyed semantic probe v1\n` is experimental (`packages/architecture/src/keyed.rs:132`); the paper's symbolic key parameter is not a target encoding or deployment. Proposed stable spelling remains `KeyedArchitectureBinding { profile, schema_version, digest }`; set `KEYED_SCHEMA_VERSION = 2`, domain bytes `b"tripod keyed provenance covenant manifest v2\n"`, and explicitly encode `KeyParameterId::CovenantKey` as code 1 instead of leaving the singleton type to imply it. After the domain, encode schema as big-endian u32, profile and key-parameter codes as big-endian u16, length-framed UTF-8 paper path and paper version (u64 byte lengths), the 32-byte validated anchor-set hash, then sorted objects, operations and clauses with u64 row counts and u16 namespace/local codes (clause namespace zero); hash these bytes with SHA-256. Preserve existing profile/local identifier codes and paper version `1.0.0` (`packages/architecture/src/semantic.rs:10`, `papers/lineage/main.tex:45`), and keep attestation schema 18 and all its canonical bytes/hashes untouched. Acceptance at bite 1.1: review this exact grammar, pin independently expected keyed bytes/digest, permutation invariance, malformed profile/schema/paper/census refusals and the explicit singleton-key encoding pin, and refusal of an old experimental binding; an anchor hash is a paper-label binding, not a content hash of all paper text. Reopen at the candidate audit on ambiguous framing, any future admitted manifest field missing from the identity, or a paper/semantic change requiring a new keyed version. This study assigns no digest value and no network/wire format.

**Proposal (174 — Owner location and visibility)** · `proposal:w1home:owners`

174. Keep declaration ownership in architecture/realization and compiler-bound owners in compiler; retain `compiler::input::AttestationOwner` and the eventual stable `compiler::input::KeyedOwner` as documented opaque public types because they occur in `BoundCompilerInput<O>`, with sealed binding and no public construction or lowering hooks. Reason: the owner retains compiler scope/policy-facing source, so moving it into realization would invert dependencies; hiding a type still exposed as a default generic would not remove the API obligation (`packages/compiler/src/input.rs:15`, `packages/compiler/src/input.rs:118`, `packages/compiler/src/input.rs:263`). Proposed physical home for the owner structs is `packages/compiler/src/input/owner.rs`, with owner-specific lowering implementations remaining private under `engine_attestation` and `engine_keyed`; that mechanical move belongs with bite 1.2, not a new crate. Architecture's manifest validation and realization's sealed `OperationDeclaration` remain the earlier ownership boundaries (`packages/realization/src/declarations/mod.rs:25`). Acceptance: default/feature public-item inventory, external binding type-mismatch controls and no public engine-record payload or callback. Reopen at the candidate audit on a dependency cycle, downstream need for unsealed owners, public construction bypass, or genuinely independent consumers needing a lower-level owner package.

**Proposal (175 — Production keyed analysis and feature boundary)** · `proposal:w1home:analysis`

Stabilize architecture/realization keyed declaration APIs in bite 1.1, but move keyed whole-program analysis out of `cfg(test)` in bite 1.2 before claiming compiled keyed plans; do not expose the raw record structs or simply ungate the test oracle. Reason: the present feature only supplies binding, while production preparation/publication is attestation-specific (`packages/compiler/src/analyzed.rs:238`, `packages/compiler/src/analyzed.rs:259`, `packages/compiler/src/engine_attestation/program.rs:311`). Compiler keyed binding/unfinished analysis may remain under `keyed-experimental` until the bite-1.2 gate, then publish the stable typed plan entry through the normal library surface; no declaration-stability claim implies that an experimental compiler binding already compiles plans. Acceptance: a non-`cfg(test)` library/integration consumer runs SOURCE, MERGE and both through all seven stages, repeats both-direction leakage and all stage/placement/coverage negatives and full fixed-owner re-derivation, with the ten attestation pins unchanged. Reopen at the candidate audit on a missing production stage, duplicated analyzer, owner recovery, lost SOURCE evidence, or an empty stage not derived and revalidated from declarations.
