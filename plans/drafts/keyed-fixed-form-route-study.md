## 1. Design premises · `sec:kffroute:positions`

| Position | Engineering reason and precise scope |
|---|---|
| P1. One template binds the public triple of key K, asset A and value v; output zero of every extension has explicit A and explicit v. Different A or v means a different covenant. | Public amounts remove the unresolved fully confidential continuation problem. A correctly formed single handle retains one fixed deposit at its successor; its extender cannot reduce that deposit. SOURCE membership is addressed separately below. |
| P2. No accounting or authorization clause is added. | Compare fields with deployment constants, not sums or input/output resource equations. The covenant neither asks for signatures nor constrains surplus destinations. |
| P3. All three coordinates are deployment data. | K separates systems, A identifies incidental funding, v fixes its deposit/incentive. Neither this plan nor the paper selects a deployment's bytes or amount. Test representatives are evidence subjects, not template parameters fixed in code. |
| P4. The minimum is a labelled source-derived figure confirmed natively; the default is a small multiple of it. | A bracket cannot establish the rounding rule, serialized size or operator-rate dependence. Use the formula at (`calc:kffroute:dust-minimum`), with its policy-asset and nonce qualifications. |
| P5. Merging n correctly formed handles releases `(n−1)v` of A, unconstrained by the leaf. | A small deposit roughly funds marginal gathering; a larger deployment-selected deposit deliberately rewards gathering. No individual consumed deposit remains owned or separately conserved. |
| P6. This is a new major paper and realization. | Leaf, program and addresses change. Existing program equality prevents old handles migrating into the new program. Old observations remain evidence of old bytes; new claims require new observations. |

FOLLOWS — Public extension means construction from public authenticated history and the extending party's own funding/ordinary signatures, subject to inclusion and policy, not free or guaranteed inclusion. P1 removes the extender's asset and value form choice; the nonce remains extender-chosen and affects only serialized size and policy (`elements: src/primitives/confidential.h:160`). The nonce can carry bytes outside covenant identity. P1 establishes neither fairness nor bounded contention nor spendability of arbitrary payments to a program. P5 qualifies the single-handle deposit statement in P1 rather than contradicting it.

## 2. Source facts and floor · `sec:kffroute:facts`

READ — The current leaf is 52 bytes: `20 K 75 B P 51`, with ten-byte birth `B = cd c7 75 04 00000000 88 75` and seven-byte continuation `P = cd ca 00 d1 7b 88 88`. It compares only program/version (`packages/tapscript/src/keyed_pattern.rs:20–30,90–117`), recorded at (`rec:w1b13:resources`). Asset/value form was left open (`plans/backlog.md:472`); other assets/confidential envelopes are outside observation (`sec:w1b16:packet-domain`).

READ — Amount validation parses input commitments without asking for openings, checks a Pedersen tally, requires output range proofs for confidential values and surjection proofs for confidential assets (`elements: src/confidential_validation.cpp:174–209,333–419`). Range-proof verification binds the value commitment, asset generator and script, not outpoint, output index or transaction (`elements: src/script/sigcache.cpp:131–163`). Fees require empty script and explicit asset/value (`elements: src/primitives/transaction.h:324–326`).

FOLLOWS — The old explicit-asset/confidential-value handle can be publicly continued by copying commitment, asset, script and range proof, with independent fee sponsorship: its commitment cancels in the tally and the same proof statement verifies. Confidential value alone therefore is not a lock. Confidential-asset/explicit-value also has an algebraic route: change generator A to A+tG, keep known v, prove difference t, and compensate v*t in known sponsor-change blinding. These are constructions from the predicates, not native observations.

READ — A surjection proof selecting an unchanged generator creates an infinity public key and fails (`elements: src/secp256k1/src/modules/surjection/surjection_impl.h:66–80`; `elements: src/secp256k1/src/modules/rangeproof/borromean_impl.h:77–78`). OPEN — Fully confidential public continuation remains unresolved; failing that copy construction is not an impossibility theorem. The new explicit domain avoids relying on a result about it. No creator-only theorem is claimed.

**Derivation (Empty-nonce policy-asset minimum)** · `calc:kffroute:dust-minimum`

READ — Elements CTxOut serializes asset, value, nonce and script (`elements: src/primitives/transaction.h:276–286`). Confidential-field serialization adds a prefix and its payload, without length framing (`elements: src/primitives/confidential.h:27–35`): explicit asset 33 bytes, explicit value nine, absent nonce one (`elements: src/primitives/confidential.h:100,130,160`). The script is a byte prevector (`elements: src/script/script.h:471–476`), with CompactSize then bytes (`elements: src/serialize.h:308–312,823–827`). A witness-v1 32-byte program is a 34-byte script. FOLLOWS — `S_out = 33+9+1+1+34 = 78` bytes; the leaf's length does not enlarge the output program.

READ — `GetDustThreshold` adds `32+4+1+(107/WITNESS_SCALE_FACTOR)+4` to that output size for witness programs; `IsDust` compares explicit value strictly below the result (`elements: src/policy/policy.cpp:48–79`). `WITNESS_SCALE_FACTOR=4` (`elements: src/consensus/consensus.h:21`). `DUST_RELAY_TX_FEE=100` (`elements: src/policy/policy.h:66`) initializes `MemPoolOptions::dust_relay_feerate` (`elements: src/kernel/mempool_options.h:45`); `-dustrelayfee` overrides it (`elements: src/node/mempool_args.cpp:74–79`; option at `elements: src/init.cpp:650`). Standardness consumes that option (`elements: src/validation.cpp:944,1157`). `CFeeRate::GetFee` uses ceiling, with a nonzero fallback (`elements: src/policy/feerate.cpp:23–36`).

FOLLOWS — Define `D(R,S) = CFeeRate(R).GetFee(S + 32+4+1+floor(107/WITNESS_SCALE_FACTOR)+4)` and `M(R,S)=max(1,D(R,S))`. For positive R and the inspected shape, `D(R,78)=ceil(145R/1000)`; at the named default it is 15 smallest policy-asset units. One is the separate spendable consensus floor (`elements: src/confidential_validation.cpp:333–343`); 15 is a policy figure, not a paper-wide constant. The admissible individual amount range is `1 ≤ v ≤ MAX_MONEY`; aggregate explicit outputs must also remain in MoneyRange (`elements: src/consensus/tx_check.cpp:23–37`). The recorded 10-refused/100-admitted bracket is consistent but nonadjacent, with 11–99 untested (`rec:state-constructor:keyed-funding`, `plans/research/state-constructor.md:684`). The new capture must test the derived adjacent edge.

READ — `GetDust` skips explicit non-policy assets (`elements: src/policy/policy.cpp:88`). FOLLOWS — Their consensus positive floor is one, but this policy rule supplies no further floor in their units; policy-asset fees still need funding. A present nonce adds 32 bytes, giving S=110 and `ceil(177R/1000)`, or 18 at the inspected default. Recommend an empty-nonce constructor envelope without adding nonce identity to the triple: a future extender can clear a nonce, but the 15-unit relay claim is only for the named shape.

READ — Ephemeral policy requires dust transactions to have zero base/modified fee and requires a child to sweep parent dust (`elements: src/policy/ephemeral_policy.cpp:23–30,49–90`); its header permits the zero-fee/no-child case and describes mining disincentives, not a consensus ban (`elements: src/policy/ephemeral_policy.h:18–39`). FOLLOWS — It is not a normal fee-paying resting endpoint exception: a fee-paying child must consume the dust handle, and a child recreating fixed dust is itself zero-fee. For one dust output, `IsStandardTx` allows the count (`elements: src/policy/policy.cpp:177–181`; maximum one at `elements: src/policy/policy.h:94`). A positive-fee subject instead fails `PreCheckEphemeralTx` with reason `dust` and debug text "tx with dust output must be 0-fee" (`elements: src/policy/ephemeral_policy.cpp:26–27`). A zero-fee subject passes these dust checks and faces fee/package checks (`elements: src/validation.cpp:1169–1184,1812–1848`); no absolute mining impossibility follows.

**Proposal (Default formula)** · `proposal:kffroute:default`

Recommend `v_default = 4*M(R_reference,S_reference)` for a policy-asset deployment with the empty-nonce envelope. FOLLOWS — The inspected example yields 60, but 60 is not installed as a deployment constant. Such an amount tolerates integer dust rates through `floor(1000v/145)`, 413 in this example; fourfold rate 400 yields threshold 58. Non-null nonce changes the denominator to 177. Policy changes require a newly stated envelope, not silently changing an existing covenant's v/address.

READ — The old marginal input is 255 WU = 41 base bytes times four +91 witness bytes; observed fee floor is 100 units/kvB (`plans/research/state-constructor.md:696–709`). FOLLOWS — A leaf of L bytes below the CompactSize boundary gives full keyed witness L+39 and marginal weight L+203. For the recommended 104-byte leaf below, 307 WU has conservative marginal fee `ceil(ceil(307/4)*100/1000)=8`; actual whole-transaction rounding, count framing and signatures still matter. The illustrative release of 60 per additional handle leaves roughly 52 after that marginal allowance, not a guarantee that every merge funds its fixed overhead. Non-policy-asset surplus is not interchangeable with policy fees. OPEN — New weights, policy limits and actual timings require capture.

## 3. Encoding and membership routes · `sec:kffroute:routes`

READ — Asset/value introspection pushes payload then prefix; explicit value payload is eight-byte little-endian, despite big-endian wire serialization (`elements: src/script/interpreter.cpp:98–121`). Asset explicit prefix is 01, confidential 0a/0b; value confidential prefixes are 08/09 (`elements: src/primitives/confidential.h:100,130`). Below A means those exact 32 asset payload bytes and V means LE64(v); it does not assume explorer display byte order.

| Route | Exact construction and predicted cost | What it leaves open / disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Literal output constants | FOLLOWS — Full leaf `20 K 75 20 A 08 V 00 cf 51 88 88 00 ce 51 88 88 B P 51`: +52 =104 bytes; peak five. Value equality consumes V, then asset equality consumes A. | Direct byte inspection, no new hash predicate; malformed SOURCE form remains unconstrained. Valid alternative. |
| Literal constants at both ends | FOLLOWS — After A,V, `6e` duplicates the pair; check output as above, then `cd c9 51 88 88 cd c8 51 88 88`, before B,P,true. +63 =115 bytes; peak seven. | Refuses malformed SOURCE spending without input/output accounting; larger witnesses. Valid alternative. |
| Inherited explicit pair | FOLLOWS — Add `cd c8 51 88 00 ce 51 88 88` and the analogous c9/cf guard: +18 =70 bytes. | Reject: different SOURCE pairs have the same program and cannot necessarily merge. It violates P1's triple identity and P2's constant-only comparison unless another program-binding mechanism is added, losing its advertised simplicity. |
| Hash of explicit pair | FOLLOWS — T=A concatenated with 01,V,01; H=SHA256(T). Let `O=00 ce 7e c4 00 cf 7e c6`, `I=cd c8 7e c4 cd c9 7e c6`. Output-only `20 K 75 20 H O 88 B P 51` is 94 bytes; both-ends `20 K 75 20 H 76 O 88 I 88 B P 51` is 104 bytes, peak five. | Recommend both ends. Prefixes bind representation. Public API carries K,A,v, not an unexplained digest. Requires SHA-256 assumptions and independent transcript tests. |
| Packed or small-number literals | FOLLOWS — A 42-byte transcript push plus CAT/equality yields 103 bytes output-only; replacing nine-byte V push by `01 3c e0` for illustrative 60 gives 98 bytes. | Packed saving is slight; numeric optimization has a bounded script-number domain and variable grammar. Prefer uniform hash recipe. |

READ — CAT is 7e, streaming SHA-256 initialize/finalize are c4/c6 and are available in the pinned interpreter (`elements: src/script/interpreter.cpp:1023–1041,1736–1751,1777–1799`) and reviewed in `packages/target-elements/src/opcode.rs:947–987,1996–2014`. DUP/2DUP are reviewed at `packages/target-elements/src/opcode.rs:1854–1862`. A one-shot a8 hash has the same byte count but is not in that reviewed census. FOLLOWS — Streaming therefore avoids expanding the target primitive census. Hash equality is conditional on collision/second-preimage resistance, not unconditional distinctness of all triples.

FOLLOWS — Duplicate H; O/equality consumes one copy; I/equality consumes the other, emptying the stack. Both compare with a constant. The leaf has 36 instructions/29 primitive calls, 140 script-stack witness bytes, 143 full input-witness bytes, budget 190 and 33-byte control. Streaming context width 73 replaces 40 (`elements: src/crypto/sha256.cpp:772–787`); peak five/width 73 fit limits 1000/520 (`elements: src/script/script.h:32,47`). Modeled hash/CAT validation charge is zero (`packages/target-elements/src/opcode.rs:879–887`), not measured CPU. Reopen projections and the old 1564-input bound (`tab:w1b13:resource-formulas`).

**Proposal (SOURCE and handle membership)** · `proposal:kffroute:membership`

Recommend first-position, authenticated triple program AND exact explicit A,v as fixed-form membership; guard both input and successor. A malformed payment is creatable but is a non-handle/no SOURCE record, whose script refuses spending regardless of openings. Born-first/continue-first clauses then use triple-qualified membership; expose input qualification explicitly.

READ — Current first-position program membership makes a SOURCE singleton; filtering changes acceptance, not membership (`papers/lineage/sections/02_model.tex:18–44`; `papers/lineage/sections/01_interface.tex:29–37,55–79`). FOLLOWS — Input guards alone strand those records. Output-only guards allow public normalization of explicit malformed SOURCEs, possibly releasing excess; some confidential forms can pass through later same-script outputs, but fully confidential normalization remains OPEN. Preserve legacy SOURCE readings under legacy rules.

**Proposal (Paper claims and reader scope)** · `proposal:kffroute:paper`

State the constant predicate, no accounting/authorization, revised safety/reading, `(n−1)v` release and unspendability of every nonmember program-bearing output, malformed first-position forms included. Under the sole-leaf/NUMS premises its value is permanently locked, with no SOURCE record or attributable destruction record. Retrieval must supply authenticated asset/value fields and forms for each queried, SOURCE and recursively reached handle as well as complete input classification; reconstruction checks the pair and returns "not a handle" for malformed first outputs. This extends `papers/lineage/sections/01_interface.tex:44–45,56–66` and generalizes single birth at `papers/lineage/sections/02_model.tex:47–53`. Unique provenance, continuation, growth, separation and faithful reading are re-proved over the new class, not discarded; separation retains the conditional distinct-program premise. Extension needs public triple/recipe/control, authenticated history/parents, own funding/signatures, consensus-valid pair and declared relay envelope. Inclusion/finality remain premises; absence of covenant signatures promises no bounded racing/waiting or fees.

FOLLOWS — Full reading needs dependency closure; a chain witness supplies a growing ancestry path, not omitted branches or a complete set. Measure existing full-reader cost; defer a new chain API. MERGE adds no record and does not change earlier readings. If acceptance requires attributable destruction, it belongs in the SOURCE event. Keyed provenance imposes no destruction and is separate from attestation Burn/Clear.
