> **Status:** Chartered execution guide of Phase 7; Wave 0 is the wave this charter authorizes, and opens on its entry record when its entry obligations, including the provisioned pinned target on the node of record, are recorded in the backlog; implementation of new semantics is held until Wave 0's exit.

> **Affected packages:** `architecture`, `realization`, `compiler`, `target-elements`, `tapscript`, `linker`, `transaction`, and `vectors`; `target-elements-conformance` only for a demonstrated missing target-generic fact; `model` supplies independent conformance evidence and `labels` checks registrations.

> **Trust boundary:** Repository source and selected executors run only within the established execution trust boundary; public disposable fixtures retain the security requirements of `adr/015-public-data-and-execution-trust.md`.

## 1. Why this order · `sec:guide15-exec:order`

New semantics composed on a base with 128 outstanding Safety rows and an unrefit region model inherit both limits; a later failure would have two candidate causes, the inherited boundary or the new operation (`sec:phase6:exit-assessment`).

The region refit is exercised by the first composed operation, so it precedes every new operation rather than becoming a repair attempted after a composed failure (`T11-064`).

The 35 native-run-required rows and the second exemplar's measurements need the same pinned target on the node of record; one provisioned environment supports both, while each observation retains its own exact subject and evidence boundary.

A debt wave with its own exit turns the inherited figures into an explicit baseline; later waves compare their figures against that baseline instead of treating a historical green gate as an answer to a new question.

The second exemplar's two declarations test the architecture-to-realization boundary with the smallest contract before the larger Phase-7 declarations; its requirements and package mapping are stated in `plans/drafts/destruction-lineage-exemplar-study.md` §§2–6.

Its measured carrier forms supply concrete precedent for the burn-record carrier question without choosing an attestation record law by analogy (`q:guide15:burn-record-carrier`).

An independence failure is cheapest to find before burn and clear depend on the same seams; the result must name which seams moved, why they moved, and which laws remained specific to each contract.

The phase order does not change: the second contract is independence evidence inside Phase 7, admitted by the phase's change statement (`sec:phase7:change-statement`).

The Class column states both the source kind, condition bullet or repository-record field, and one of inherited policy, structural implementation, observed candidate evidence, or new Phase-7 choice.

Finding status records what the audit establishes, while execution status records work still required for this guide; HOLDS is never a fresh native observation or a completed future implementation.

### Semantic entry without a circular prerequisite

Known model law and complete field ownership are Wave-0 obligations; typed compiler-facing burn and clear declarations are Wave-2 obligations, so the two DOES NOT HOLD rows retain that finding and receive this explicit execution disposition (`gate:guide15:semantic-entry`).

Wave 0 must identify the authoritative relation and assignment censuses and their later validation obligations; it does not claim that unsupported realization dispatch has become supported merely because the model already defines the operations.

The distinction applies row by row to burn records, STATE assignment, public availability, constructor continuity, signing selection, and report roles; later evidence is required at the consuming wave before any implementation result is accepted.

### The twelve controlling dispositions

1. Semantic census: bite 0.7 records exact typed owners for burn, ASH, clear, STATE, records, events, accounting, and certificates; Wave 2 delivers missing realization declarations and conformance (`q:guide15:semantic-census`).
2. ASH representation: retain the accepted explicit-only public boundary and revalidate it in bite 0.7; public commitments need separately accepted authenticated-opening evidence (`q:guide15:ash-representation`).
3. Private burn: normalized-first is mandatory; direct-private support remains absent unless its distinct admission obligations are met and recorded (`q:guide15:private-burn`).
4. ASH constructor: bite 0.7 records exact intended equality or explicit class transitions; Wave 5 proves fresh, compacted, and residual equality on actual linked subjects (`q:guide15:ash-constructor`).
5. Burn-record carrier: bite 0.7 selects a canonical, publicly recoverable carrier with exact association and ordering; Wave-1 form measurements are later regression evidence and cannot substitute for this preflight disposition (`q:guide15:burn-record-carrier`).
6. Burn-record rule: bite 0.7 derives zero/count/ordinal/duplicate/value/aggregate rules from typed authority, retaining any rule stronger than a minimum inequality (`q:guide15:burn-record-rule`).
7. Clear assignment: the stored assignment changes `y_l` alone by X; total Y is derived and falls by X, with other stored fields unchanged; Wave 2 must validate that distinction (`q:guide15:clear-assignment`).
8. Arithmetic: bite 0.7 records widths, domains, sum bounds, overflow refusal, branch schedule, consumed comparisons, and authenticated minimum operands for addition, subtraction, and comparison (`q:guide15:clear-arithmetic`).
9. STATE generation: bite 0.7 records synthetic Phase-7 origin, maturity-plus-clear subtree, no generation crossing, maturity revalidation, and no migration claim, or an explicitly accepted migration prerequisite (`q:guide15:state-generation`).
10. Event independence: keep operation acceptance, event type, record value, compact silence, clear event, accounting, and STATE succession as separate validated roles (`q:guide15:event-independence`).
11. Public construction: bite 0.7 defines fresh-process tests with public chain data, bundle, ABI, values, metadata and constructor data; optional sponsor-local capability cannot hide creator dependencies (`q:guide15:permissionless`).
12. Query boundary: internal record and event projection is the default; a named external consumer activates ADR-022 before an interface or identity is implemented (`q:guide15:attestation-query`).

## 4. Wave 1 — The second exemplar: destruction lineage · `task:guide15-exec:wave1`

Wave 1 opens only on the Wave-0 exit and carries the contract in `plans/drafts/destruction-lineage-exemplar-study.md` §§2–6 and §9 through declaration, analysis, emission, linking, construction, and translation-validation evidence.

The contract destroys a positive explicit native amount in an OP_RETURN output containing exactly one opaque 32-byte address; its magnitude is consensus value, not a quantity encoded in the address data.

BURN creates the minimal-value claim token at output 0 beside the destruction output at output 1; the pair fixes association without constraining unrelated inputs, outputs, authorization, or carrier purpose beyond target validity.

MERGE consumes one or more outputs carrying the fixed covenant and produces output 0 carrying the same covenant; each covenant input checks only that successor script, without a signature, value comparison, asset comparison, or closure over other outputs.

The designated genesis outpoint contributes no destruction record; a descendant lineage point settles the set of valid token-outpoint records in its ancestry, including a newly valid pair when BURN and MERGE coincide.

Side piles may merge into the designated head later, but their depth does not designate the canonical lineage; record identity prevents duplicate credit through shared ancestry, and malformed pairs contribute nothing.

Consumer acceptance obeys A(previous) ⊆ A(this) ⊆ L(this), with eligibility, timing, and boundary recognition in the consumer's published rule rather than the covenant; a cached commitment has no settlement authority.

The covenant carries no amount accumulator, count, index, aggregate commitment, ownership role, or redemption entitlement; incidental funding is not record magnitude and no third operation is introduced.

### 1.0 Measure the six target obligations

The realization's own documentation must argue each of the four traps using its own laws rather than a borrowed assurance claim.

- Accumulator trap: no total, count, index, or commitment is stored; ancestry reconstruction computes figures and does not remove head contention (`[RZ-trap:architecture:accumulator]`).

## 6. Wave gates and the baseline rule · `rule:guide15-exec:baseline`

A wave opens only on the previous wave's exit gate; Wave 0 is the initial exception to predecessor numbering and opens only on its own backlog entry record with every entry obligation recorded.

Every wave's gate record states its figures beside the Wave-0 baseline; a regression against that baseline is a finding requiring an explicit disposition before advancement, not an adjustment hidden in a new total.

The baseline records exact target, deployment, policy, candidate, protocol, constructor generation, evidence corpus, commands, clean-tree state, and measured dimensions so that comparisons have matching subjects.

Each record distinguishes answered rows, typed retirements, still-required evidence, and inapplicable claims; a retyped row never becomes an observed refusal merely because it leaves an outstanding bucket.

The Safety census, relation-link census, four backlog carriers, five conjunct dispositions, compact negative matrix, twelve questions, and 65 entry rows remain separately countable throughout the later waves.

An altered target, deployment, policy, constructor, ABI, or protocol requires an explicit compatibility argument and rerun of the affected evidence; a historical success does not automatically cover the new subject.

Each wave retains focused verification for its changed packages and relation boundaries, then the repository gate required by its exit; the final batch gate remains (`gate:guide15:batch`) and Phase 7 exits only at (`gate:phase7:exit`).

Gate records state commands, observed results, exit codes, durations, retained logs, and absent dimensions; a rendered report, successful process start, or returned control without completion evidence is not a passing run.

An inherited deliverable remains in the consuming wave's inventory with its source and exact reuse argument; changed premises require revalidation rather than deletion of the obligation.

The complete result matrix records burn, compact, clear, representation, constructor, event, accounting, public construction, coverage, and resources independently (`tab:guide15:result`); second-contract evidence adds independence and replaces none of those rows.

## 7. What this guide does not claim · `sec:guide15-exec:non-claims`

The accepted Phase-6 closeout is unchanged at its amended scope, with its original §23 verdict, outstanding-row figures, candidate boundaries, and finite evidence corpus preserved as historical facts (`sec:phase6:exit-assessment`).

No production custody, production deployment, final calibration, release, or general theorem beyond the finite admitted corpus is claimed; the lack of independent chain observation is an inherited entry limitation that bite 0.5 must discharge for its exact observed subject.

Neither the charter nor a planned target run proves unavailable evidence; no missing-node explanation can satisfy the Wave-0 exit, and no observation can discharge the NUMS discrete-log impossibility claim.

The second contract supplies independence evidence inside Phase 7; it introduces no third operation, aggregate covenant equation, incidental-value preservation, carrier-output closure, or consumer authorization in the covenant.

Its exact funding floor, input bound, relay forms, replacement premises, recurrence costs, historical availability, and finality remain bound to measured subjects and published assumptions, never universal deployment guarantees.

The roadmap's thesis is unchanged: the attestation remains the exemplar by which the compiler is accepted, and the second contract tests independence inside the phase under (`sec:phase7:change-statement`); no phase dependency or normative version changes through this charter.

The concept remains unchanged as semantic reference, the two studies remain planning input, and this execution guide cannot override a package contract, phase card, ADR, or accepted decision (`rule:guides:authority`); (`rule:drafts:authority`).
