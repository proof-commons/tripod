## Public parameters and exact qualification · `sec:keyed-fixed:qualification`

K is exactly 32 public opaque bytes, including zero; it is neither a scalar nor a signing key. A is exactly the 32-byte explicit asset payload in the target's authenticated representation, not an assumed explorer display order. v is a positive explicit amount admitted by the target: `1 ≤ v ≤ MAX_MONEY`, with the separate transaction-wide MoneyRange checks retained. These widths follow packages/tapscript/src/keyed_constructor.rs and the field transcription at (`[PLAN-sec:kffroute:routes]`); the amount domain follows `elements: src/confidential_validation.cpp:333–343` and `src/consensus/tx_check.cpp:23–37`, cited at (`[PLAN-calc:kffroute:dust-minimum]`). No numeric MAX_MONEY is supplied by that derivation. A deployment authenticates its chosen triple throughout construction, reading and evidence; no example below chooses it.

An output is a member precisely when it is at birth position zero, has the authenticated program Pq reconstructed from that triple, and has explicit asset A and explicit value v (`[A-def:kpcfixed-model:keyed-membership]`). Nonce is not membership. An ordinary payment can create a wrong-pair or wrong-form first output, or a correct-pair later output; these are nonmembers with no record set. Their creation does not execute the leaf. Own-qualification and born-first respectively prevent their subsequent spending under the cryptographic premises below, even by an opening holder.

The spending law is exactly born-first, own-qualification and successor qualification at output zero (`[A-def:kpcfixed-model:first-output]`). The program comparison is authenticated current-program equality, which compares with Pq because the spent program is Pq; asset and value at each end compare with the constant transcript of A,v. There is no input/output amount comparison, sum, counter, authorization, destruction, terminal or designated head. Other inputs and outputs remain subject to ordinary transaction validity. SOURCE consumes no handle of its own triple, may consume ordinary funding inputs, and introduces its handle's record (`[A-op:kpcfixed-operations:source]`); MERGE of n members retains v and makes `(n−1)v = nv−v` available without constraining its destination or introducing a record (`[A-prop:kpcfixed-operations:merge-surplus]`). Release is not reader credit and need not cover fees.

## Leaf and commitment recipe · `sec:keyed-fixed:recipe`

S5 implements this schedule in packages/tapscript/src/keyed_fixed_pattern.rs and links its full occurrence census in packages/linker/src/keyed_fixed_bundle.rs. Independent record-assembled transcript, byte, stack and commitment checks are packages/vectors/tests/keyed_fixed_template.rs; their expectations do not call the emitter. Model refusals cover both asset/value forms and missing successor, birth and program/version sites. These are offline controls; S8 still owes native execution, opening-holder and key-path subjects. The new freeze admission and S8 deadline are (`[PLAN-rec:identities:keyed-fixed-capture-stop]`).

All hex tokens below denote literal bytes; concatenation preserves order. Let `V=LE64(v)`, `T=A || 01 || V || 01` and `H=SHA256(T)`. T is exactly `32+1+8+1=42` bytes. The prefixes are part of the hash: confidential asset prefixes 0a/0b and value prefixes 08/09 do not encode the explicit pair. Introspection pushes payload then prefix; explicit value introspection is little-endian although wire value serialization is big-endian. The source transcription is `elements: src/script/interpreter.cpp:98–121`, `src/primitives/confidential.h:100,130` at (`[PLAN-sec:kffroute:routes]`). Public callers supply K,A,v, not an unauthenticated opaque H.

```text
B = cd c7 75 04 00000000 88 75
P = cd ca 00 d1 7b 88 88
O = 00 ce 7e c4 00 cf 7e c6
I = cd c8 7e c4 cd c9 7e c6
leaf = 20 K 75 20 H 76 O 88 I 88 B P 51
```

K and H each occupy 32 bytes after their minimal push32. K is dropped. DUP retains two copies of constant H. O inspects successor zero's asset and value; CAT appends each prefix to its payload, streaming SHA-256 initializes on asset-plus-prefix and finalizes with value-plus-prefix. EQUALVERIFY consumes one H. I does the same for the executing input, consuming the other H. B inspects that input's authenticated outpoint, drops flags, compares its output index with four zero bytes, then drops the transaction identifier. This is birth position, not executing input index zero; flags are not a covenant predicate. P compares the current and first successor witness versions and program payloads, then final true leaves the sole successful stack item. A missing successor fails introspection. No witness argument or signature is required by the leaf.

B and P are transcribed from packages/tapscript/src/keyed_pattern.rs; the adopted O/I schedule and streaming/CAT meanings are (`[PLAN-sec:kffroute:routes]`) and packages/target-elements/src/opcode.rs. The latter reviews CAT 7e and streaming initialize/finalize c4/c6 against `elements: src/script/interpreter.cpp:1023–1041,1736–1751,1777–1799`. Leaf-version c4 is a separate byte role from opcode c4. S5 must independently verify the complete instruction/transcript/stack/linker census; this transcription grants no old linker's fixed-position grammar permission to accept a new leaf.

The leaf version is c4 (`LeafVersion::TAPSCRIPT` in packages/target-elements/src/opcode.rs). Use the sole leaf at depth zero, with no sibling, branch, alternative leaf or caller-chosen version. The public internal x coordinate is SHA256 of the 65-byte uncompressed secp256k1 generator `04 || Gx || Gy`, lifted with even y. The generator coordinates and comparison literal are `STATE_GENERATOR_X`, `STATE_GENERATOR_Y`, `STATE_NUMS_KEY` in packages/tapscript/src/state_constructor.rs; packages/transaction/src/keyed_taproot.rs recomputes the hash. These source constants and the derivation, rather than a deployment-supplied internal key, determine the internal point R.

Define `tagged(tag,m)=SHA256(SHA256(tag) || SHA256(tag) || m)`, using the ASCII tag bytes. The root is `tagged("TapLeaf/elements", c4 || CompactSize(length(leaf)) || leaf)`, whose length byte is 68 for 104 bytes. Interpret `tagged("TapTweak/elements", internal_x || root)` as a big-endian integer t. Require t below secp256k1's group order n, without reduction; zero is valid. Require the finite point `Q=R+tG`; non-liftable internal points, overflowing tweaks and infinity refuse commitment. No retry, rehash or nonce search changes the triple. The output script is `51 20 || x(Q)`, 34 bytes. The control is `(c4 OR parity(Q)) || internal_x`, 33 bytes, with no siblings. These rules follow the public recipe in packages/tapscript/src/keyed_constructor.rs and commitment in packages/transaction/src/keyed_taproot.rs; the leaf body is the fixed-form replacement specified here. Successful commitment for every syntactically valid triple is not claimed; a refused triple has no admitted Pq under this realization.

### Authentication premises and proof boundary · `sec:keyed-fixed:assumptions`

The public NUMS derivation is not proof that nobody knows a discrete logarithm. Taproot retains its key path: no usable signing secret is assumed under NUMS/discrete-log and SHA-256 preimage hardness. Sole-leaf authentication, fixed-triple separation and absence of substituted programs additionally depend on the target commitment rules and SHA-256 second-preimage resistance; adversarially chosen pairs require collision resistance, including the pair transcript hash. The explicit T has fixed width; the asset part has 32 payload bytes plus its prefix, while a value payload has 8 bytes when explicit or 32 when confidential, with its form prefix included. A null value introspects as eight zero bytes with prefix 01 (`elements: src/script/interpreter.cpp:108–111`), aliasing explicit zero rather than obtaining a distinct form byte. Null output values are refused by `VerifyAmounts` (`src/confidential_validation.cpp:351–355`), and the required v≥1 independently excludes that zero transcript. A null asset has no defined successful introspection mapping: both asset opcodes call pushasset (`elements: src/script/interpreter.cpp:1846–1848,1929–1931`), which asserts non-null and pushes the stored payload and prefix without a null conversion (`src/script/interpreter.cpp:98–103`). Deserializing null clears the commitment, whereas explicit A=zero has a 33-byte commitment beginning with 01 (`src/primitives/confidential.h:43–50,66–83,100`); IsValid alone permits null. VerifyAmounts rejects null spent-input assets directly (`src/confidential_validation.cpp:173–178`) and rejects output assets outside explicit/commitment forms (`src/confidential_validation.cpp:310–331`). Thus the authenticated valid-transaction domain excludes null assets; no null-asset alias to explicit zero is provided by the interpreter. This source premise does not claim safe evaluation of an invalid null-asset object supplied outside that domain. These framing, validity and positivity premises prevent a parsing or null-form alias to the admitted explicit pair; hash equality retains its cryptographic assumptions. Hashes and x-only commitments do not provide an unconditional injectivity theorem. Finite key-path refusals cannot establish impossibility of a usable secret. Secret openings do not change authenticated explicitness, birth position or the constant predicates.

## Constructor and relay envelope · `sec:keyed-fixed:envelope`

The canonical constructor uses a successfully committed triple, explicit A,v at output zero, empty nonce, the depth-zero script/control witness with no extra arguments or annex, and authenticated ordinary parents without issuance or peg-in. It requires the extender's own adequate funding and any ordinary sponsor signatures, valid value balance and explicit fee outputs, admissible individual and aggregate amounts, and the target's effective resource and policy bounds. Empty nonce minimizes size; a consensus-valid present nonce with the exact explicit pair is still a member. Readers must not turn this constructor/policy restriction into another membership condition.

Ruling 214 at (`[PLAN-rule:phase7:fixed-form-rulings]`) declares the supported form for triples whose A is the network policy asset: SOURCE has no member, MERGE has one or more same-triple members with empty-nonce parents and the 143-byte empty-argument script/control witness; both use exactly one ordinary P2WPKH sponsor with a worst admitted 72-byte signature and compressed key. The three outputs are the exact explicit successor with empty nonce at zero, the first edition's P2WPKH sponsor change/release destination, and an explicit fee. The inherited one-asset balance and the policy-asset floor calculation delimit this constructor; a triple of another asset, or a present-nonce parent with the exact pair, remains a paper member for readers but is not constructed here. Surplus destination choice remains within that ordinary form; the covenant imposes no destination rule. Other collateral assets/forms, additional sponsors, issuance, peg-in, annex and extra witness items are unsupported by this constructor. The triple, fee rate and effective deployment policy remain supplied.

The target resource owner in packages/target-elements/src/resource.rs supplies this constructor's 400,000-WU transaction-policy ceiling. With n members, the complete stripped transaction has `239 + CompactSizeLen(n+1) + 41n` bytes, including the one sponsor and input-count prefix; nonmember witness/framing bytes are 117 and each fixed-form member adds 143. Thus `W(n)=4*(239+CompactSizeLen(n+1)+41n)+117+143n`. From n=252 the count prefix has three bytes, yielding `W(n)=1085+307n`. The greatest n within the declared ceiling is **N=1299**: `W(1299)=399878` and `W(1300)=400185`. N+1 is a typed constructor-policy refusal before bytes, not a covenant bound or native result. S8 still measures SOURCE/MERGE endpoints and effective node policy; the old 1564 and `1085+255N` do not transfer.

`reviewed_stated_amount_bound()` admits an individual explicit value through 2,100,000,000,000,000 at checked-request admission; one above receives the typed amount-range refusal. A positive explicit fee makes a complete transaction with a maximum-valued explicit successor exceed the target's running explicit-output total. That request control is therefore separate from funded valid transactions at smaller values and from aggregate refusals. Fixed-form membership excludes confidential asset/value fields, while unrelated target-valid outputs remain unconstrained by the covenant; this narrow constructor scope does not change membership.
