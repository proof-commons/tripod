\subsection{Walkthrough}
\label{sec:verification:walkthrough}
% ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Genesis: $\reservepool{0} = 1000$, $\livesupply{0} = \livesplit\genesisreserve = 500$,
$\tlocksupply{0} = 500$, $\totsupply{0} = 1000$, $\ratefloor{0} = 1.000$. Holder addresses
Alice, Bob, Carol, and the operator. Two active cycles exercise deposit, burn, redemption,
split minting, and maturity announcement. At cycle~2 the operator announces $\matcycle=12$,
so $\Delta k=10$; no monetary operation occurs before the cycle-$12$ boundary, where
maturity conversion executes.

\paragraph{Cycle 1 events (audit sequence).}
\phantomsection\label{verif:walkthrough:cycle-one}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l l c c c c@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Event} & \textbf{Address}
        & $\reservepool{}$ & $\livesupply{}$ & $\totsupply{}$ & $\ratefloor{}$ \\
    \midrule
    Deposit 200 & Alice & 1000   & 500 & 1000 & 1.000 \\
    Burn 50     & Bob   & 1000   & 450 & 950  & 1.0526 \\
    Redeem 30   & Carol & 968.42 & 420 & 920  & 1.0526 \\
    \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Alice's deposit queues to escrow ($\escrow{} = 200$). Bob's burn at $\ratefloor{} = 1.000$
sets $\attest{\mathrm{Bob}} = 50.00$ --- the index is an opaque address
(\zcref{def:interface:address-space}), the binding to any identity being the importing
layer's import-time interpretation --- and raises $\ratefloor{}$ to $1000/950 = 1.0526$.
Carol redeems 30 at $1.0526$, receiving $31.58$ reserve units, leaving $\reservepool{} =
968.42$ and $\ratefloor{} = 968.42/920 = 1.0526$ (preserved). An attempted time-locked-class
burn is rejected: the time-locked class is structurally non-burnable
(\zcref{def:model:classes}).

\paragraph{Cycle 2 boundary.}
\phantomsection\label{verif:walkthrough:boundary}
$\ratefloor{}^{-} = 968.42/920 = 1.0526$. Process escrow $\Delta\reservepool{} = 200$. Mint
$\Delta\totsupply{} = 200/1.0526 = 190.00$; split $\Delta\livesupply{} = 95.00$,
$\Delta\tlocksupply{} = 95.00$. Alice (depositor, $1-\feerate = 0.5$) receives $47.50$
live-class and $47.50$ time-locked-class; the operator ($\feerate$) receives $47.50$ and
$47.50$. Post: $\reservepool{} = 1168.42$, $\livesupply{} = 515.00$, $\tlocksupply{} =
595.00$, $\totsupply{} = 1110.00$, $\ratefloor{} = 1.0526$ (preserved,
\zcref{alg:operations:cycle-processing}).

\paragraph{Cycle 2 events.}
The operator burns $47.50$ live-class at $1.0526$: $\attest{\opidx} = 50.00$,
$\livesupply{} = 467.50$, $\totsupply{} = 1062.50$, $\ratefloor{} = 1.0997$. Alice burns $20$
at $1.0997$: $\attest{\mathrm{Alice}} = 21.99$, $\livesupply{} = 447.50$, $\totsupply{} =
1042.50$, $\ratefloor{} = 1.1208$. At cycle~2 the operator announces
$\matcycle=12=2+10$. The lead satisfies $\matlead \leq 10 \leq \matleadcap$
(\zcref{postc:maturity:announcement}). With no intervening monetary operation, the
cycle-$12$ boundary executes maturity conversion (\zcref{def:maturity:conversion}):
$\livesupply{} \gets 447.50 + 595.00 = 1042.50$, $\tlocksupply{} \gets 0$,
$\totsupply{}$ unchanged, and $\ratefloor{} = 1.1208$ (preserved).

\paragraph{Invariant checks.}
\phantomsection\label{verif:walkthrough:invariant-checks}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l c l@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Invariant} & \textbf{Observed} & \textbf{Status} \\
    \midrule
    $\ratefloor{}$ non-decreasing & $1.000 \leq 1.0526 \leq 1.0997 \leq 1.1208$ & \okmark \\
    $\ratefloor{} \leq \reservepool{}/\tlocksupply{}$ (pre-maturity) & $1.1208 \leq 1168.42/595.00$ & \okmark \\
    $\ratefloor{}$ preserved at mint & $1.0526 = 1.0526$ & \okmark \\
    $\ratefloor{}$ preserved at redeem & $1.0526 = 1.0526$ & \okmark \\
    time-locked-class burn rejected & structurally non-burnable & \okmark \\
    minimum/maximum maturity lead & $10 \leq 10 \leq 1000$ & \okmark \\
    maturity conversion preserves $\ratefloor{}$ & $1.1208 = 1.1208$ & \okmark \\
    $\attest{\mathrm{Bob}} = 50.00$ irrevocable & non-decreasing & \okmark \\
    seigniorage $\attest{\opidx} = \feerate\livesplit D$ & $0.5\cdot0.5\cdot200 = 50.00$ & \okmark \\
    \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Costliness check: Bob destroyed 50 live-class units redeemable for $50.00$ reserve units at
the instant of destruction; $\attest{\mathrm{Bob}} = 50.00$
(\zcref{thm:interface:instantaneous-costliness}).

\subsection{Timing}
\label{sec:verification:timing}
% ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A holder burning early in a cycle receives a lower $\ratefloor{}$ than one burning late;
the maximum within-cycle appreciation is $1/(1-\cycleburnfrac{k})$
(\zcref{prop:invariants:burn-order-residual}); before maturity the standing bound
(\zcref{prop:containment:time-locked-standing-bound}) also binds.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}c c c@{}}
    \toprule
    $\cycleburnfrac{}$ & $1/(1-\cycleburnfrac{})$ & Timing gap \\
    \midrule
    $0.001$ & $1.001$ & $0.1\%$ \\
    $0.01$  & $1.010$ & $1.0\%$ \\
    $0.05$  & $1.053$ & $5.3\%$ \\
    $0.10$  & $1.111$ & $11.1\%$ \\
    \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

In the walkthrough, cycle 1 burned 50 of 1000, $\cycleburnfrac{} = 0.05$, gap $5.3\%$.
Deposit timing confers no advantage: all cycle-$k$ deposits process at
$\ratefloor{}^{(k+1)-}$.

\subsection{Recycling}
\label{sec:verification:recycling}
% ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This verifies \zcref{prop:costliness:no-recycling-advantage}: fee-derived live-class units
worth $W$ reserve units at the minting floor yield operator attestation exactly $W$ whether
burned directly or recycled through $\set{\text{redeem}, \text{deposit}, \text{burn}}$.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}c c c c@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Cycle} & \textbf{Reserve in} & \textbf{Fee burned} & \textbf{Cumulative attestation} \\
    \midrule
    1        & $50.00$  & $25.00$ & $25.00$ \\
    2        & $25.00$  & $12.50$ & $37.50$ \\
    3        & $12.50$  & $6.250$ & $43.75$ \\
    $\vdots$ &          &         & $\vdots$ \\
    $\infty$ & $0$      & $0$     & $50.00$ \\
    \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The geometric series $\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} \feerate(1-\feerate)^{i-1}W = W$ recovers the
direct-burn result ($W = 50$). During bootstrapping the time-locked class is non-burnable,
so no recycling channel through it exists.
