\begin{abstract}
    \label{abst:attestation:abstract}

    The attestation contract is a monetary machine built around a single conserved
    \emph{reserve receipt} --- a redeemable claim on a full reserve pool, held in two
    redemption classes, \liveclass{} and \tlockclass{}. A \liveclass{} unit is redeemable
    at the redemption-rate floor $\ratefloor{} = \reservepool{}/\totsupply{}$, a floor that
    only ever rises (\zcref{thm:invariants:floor-non-decrease}); a \tlockclass{} unit is
    transferable but structurally non-burnable and non-redeemable until a one-time,
    irrevocably announced maturity conversion. Burning a \liveclass{} unit destroys
    redeemable value irrecoverably and leaves a public \emph{attestation} that
    \attestrecords{the destroyed reserve-value at the settlement floor} --- an immutable
    past record, non-transferable and non-redeemable, never undone. Its reserve valuation
    is pinned to the settlement preceding it in the selected canonical chronology and is
    not raised by later settlements; a chain reorganization is a change of chronology that
    may reproject that valuation upward or downward. The layer exports exactly one
    computational object --- the attestation map
    $\attest{} : \addrspace \to \R_{\geq 0}$ --- together with six \emph{specified
    properties} (non-negativity; settlement-pinned monotonicity; public auditability as
    authenticated destruction; instantaneous costliness; bootstrap capacity bound
    $\attestcap = \genesisreserve \ln(1/(1-\livesplit))$; conservative valuation) and one
    constant, the net live share $\netliveshare$. The index of $\attest{}$ is an
    \emph{opaque address}: this layer binds no address to any identity. Everything else ---
    the floor invariants, the conservation identity and its $\livesplit$-independence,
    containment, market discipline, and bounded operator seigniorage --- is internal
    structure that makes the specified properties hold.

    Two boundaries are stated rather than blurred. The attestation confers no claim
    \atthislayer{}: the destruction is real and irrecoverable here, while any usefulness is
    conferred elsewhere; this layer \emph{declines to price} the attestation rather than
    pricing it favorably, and whether a reader confers value on it is that reader's act,
    priced in that reader's layer. The specification is likewise count-free by
    construction --- no quantity here denominates per-action, and the binding of addresses
    to identities, like every action-side ratio, belongs to a reader. Separately, and at
    the boundary below rather than above, how --- or whether --- these specified properties
    are discharged in any particular deployment lies outside this layer's scope.
\end{abstract}

\begin{motto}
    \label{motto:attestation:motto}
    Destroy the receipt, keep the record --- one floor, two classes, no counts.
\end{motto}
