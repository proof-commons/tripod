The time-locked-class price is the surface handle $\confprice{}$, evaluated as
$\confprice{k}$ or $\confprice{d}$, not a local symbol.

\paragraph{What this index excludes.}
Every Part-B semantic macro appears exactly once above, with three deliberate exclusions:
the phase helpers $\bootrange$ and $\normrange$ (presentation shorthands over
$\matcycle$) and the operator subscript token $\opidx$. They render existing symbols and
introduce no quantity of their own. There are no alias macros: a second name for an
exported quantity would be indistinguishable from a second quantity in this index and in
the surface-purity rule, so none is retained.
