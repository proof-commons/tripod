\section{Verification Suite}
\label{app:kpc:verification}
Evidence for a realization must check the following statements; this appendix claims
no completed checks. The birth family covers both clauses of
\zcref{def:kpc-model:first-output} and single birth
(\zcref{prop:kpc-model:single-birth}). The continuation family covers the
same clauses at every consumed handle and
\zcref{thm:kpc-invariants:unique-successor,thm:kpc-invariants:key-isolation}.
The record family covers SOURCE, MERGE and their exhaustive recurrence
(\zcref{op:kpc-operations:source,op:kpc-operations:merge,def:kpc-operations:classification})
with provenance and disjoint growth
(\zcref{thm:kpc-invariants:source-provenance,thm:kpc-invariants:set-membership-growth}).
The reading family covers reconstruction, incomplete and invalid evidence,
the named evidence assumptions, and path reading
(\zcref{sec:kpc:interface,thm:kpc-interface:membership-correctness,cor:kpc-interface:path-reading}).
The acceptance family checks the separation in
\zcref{rem:kpc-interface:record-evidence}.
The resource family measures a realization's costs without adding a
covenant condition or claiming that this specification supplies a cost
bound (\zcref{sec:kpc:limitations}).
