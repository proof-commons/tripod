\begin{abstract}
\label{abst:kpc:abstract}
A keyed covenant requires first-position birth and continuation into a
first output bearing the same keyed covenant. An event creating a handle
is a SOURCE if it consumes no handle of the key: the new handle identifies a record and receives its
singleton set. Otherwise it is a MERGE, gathering its input sets without
introducing a record. There is no counter or accumulator. The exported
reading is membership in a handle's dependency-derived record set.
We prove single birth, unique source provenance, unique continuation,
growth with disjoint merge inputs, separation of keys and faithful
reconstruction.
\end{abstract}
