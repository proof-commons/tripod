\section{Symbol Index}
\label{app:kpc:symbols}
\begin{longtable}{@{}l p{0.68\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Symbol & Meaning \\
\midrule
$H$ & Finite valid history; prefixes are histories. \\
$e,b$ & An event and a creating event. \\
$O(e)_i$ & Output at position $i$, starting at zero. \\
$k,\ell$ & Keys fixed at covenant generation. \\
$K_k$ & Handles bearing the covenant under $k$, born first. \\
$I_k(e)$ & Handles of $K_k$ consumed by $e$. \\
$x,y,g,s,c$ & Handles; $c$ or $s$ may name a SOURCE handle. \\
$r_k(s)=(k,s)$ & The record identified by its SOURCE handle. \\
$C_k(x)$ & The dependency-derived record set of $x$. \\
$x\preceq y$ & Zero or more successor steps from $x$ to $y$. \\
\bottomrule
