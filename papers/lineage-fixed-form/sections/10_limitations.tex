\section{Limitations and Open Problems}
\label{sec:kpcfixed:limitations}
This specification makes no realization claim. Acceptance belongs to the
reader, and finality is an evidence assumption outside the covenant
(\zcref{sec:kpcfixed:interface}). The costs of a realization remain open.
The meanings and results of additional reader folds belong to their
readers (\zcref{rem:kpcfixed-operations:reader-folds}); no monotonicity or
agreement is promised for arbitrary folds. The SOURCE-only acceptance
result of \zcref{rem:kpcfixed-interface:record-evidence} holds unchanged
under its stated premises. A fold reading the full record set still needs complete dependency
closure; a path cannot stand for it
(\zcref{rem:kpcfixed-interface:dependency-closure}).

\begin{remark}[Public extension and contention]
\label{rem:kpcfixed:public-extension}
Extension by any party requires the public authenticated triple and
program recipe, including any public data required to demonstrate a valid
spend, authenticated history and live input handles, and the party's own
adequate funding and any ordinary funding signatures. The pair must be
consensus-admissible for the target. The event must fit the relay envelope
the realization declares for the target's policy limits on event form and
resources, and satisfy other inputs' conditions. Under these premises and
\zcref{assum:kpcfixed-model:realization}, the covenant asks for no further
authority or secret: its clauses can be satisfied by placing the fixed
program and explicit pair at output zero. Public construction does not
promise inclusion. If a competing extension consumes an input handle, its
live continuation carries a superset of that input handle's set by
\zcref{thm:kpcfixed-invariants:set-membership-growth}; a retry against that
continuation needs the premises again. There is no bound on lost races,
their cost or waiting time, and no fairness or replacement-policy claim.
The covenant promises no finality; a reading claimed final rests on the
Finality assumption of \zcref{sec:kpcfixed:interface}.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Value and policy]
\label{rem:kpcfixed:policy-floor}
The value's admissible floor depends on the target's consensus and relay
policy, asset and output envelope. A realization must state that envelope
and derive its floor; the floor is not a constant of this paper. A nonce
may affect size and policy without changing membership. A deployment
supplies $k,A,v$ and authenticates them throughout construction and
reading; this specification selects none of their values. A policy change
does not change an existing triple. Merge surplus is an incidental value
consequence, not record credit or a guarantee that gathering pays its costs.
\end{remark}
