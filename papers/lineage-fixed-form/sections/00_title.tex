\begin{abstract}
\label{abst:kpcfixed:abstract}
A covenant binds a public key, asset and value. A handle is a first
output bearing its program and that exact explicit pair. Spending checks
the handle and successor against those constants, with no authorization
or comparison between inputs and outputs. An event creating a handle
is a SOURCE if it consumes no handle of the triple: the new handle identifies a record and receives its
singleton set. Otherwise it is a MERGE, gathering its input sets without
introducing a record. There is no counter or accumulator. The exported
reading is membership in a handle's dependency-derived record set.
We prove single birth, unique source provenance, unique continuation,
growth with disjoint merge inputs, separation of triples, merge surplus and faithful
reconstruction.
\end{abstract}
