\section{Symbol Index}
\label{app:kpcfixed-symbols:index}
\begin{longtable}{@{}l p{0.68\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Symbol & Meaning \\
\midrule
$H$ & Finite valid history; prefixes are histories. \\
$e,b$ & An event and a creating event. \\
$O(e)_i$ & Output at position $i$, starting at zero. \\
$q,p$ & Public triples; $q=(k,A,v)$ is key, asset and positive value. \\
$P_q$ & Authenticated program of the triple. \\
$Q_q(x)$ & Program $P_q$ and exact explicit asset $A$, value $v$. \\
$K_q$ & Outputs satisfying $Q_q$, born first. \\
$I_q(e)$ & Handles of $K_q$ consumed by $e$; other inputs are not counted. \\
$x,y,g,s,c$ & Handles; $c$ or $s$ may name a SOURCE handle. \\
$r_q(s)=(q,s)$ & The record identified by its SOURCE handle. \\
$C_q(x)$ & The dependency-derived record set of $x$. \\
$x\preceq y$ & Zero or more successor steps from $x$ to $y$. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
