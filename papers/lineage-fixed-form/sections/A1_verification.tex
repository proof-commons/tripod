\section{Verification Suite}
\label{app:kpcfixed-verification:suite}
Evidence for a realization must check the following statements; this appendix claims
no completed checks. Qualification controls vary the key, asset, value
and each explicit form separately, on both spent and successor outputs.
Malformed first-position payments and later program-bearing outputs
must be classified as nonmembers with no record and refuse spending,
including attempts using private keys or secret data revealing the contents
of non-explicit fields. Distinct-triple controls exercise
\zcref{assum:kpcfixed-model:realization,prop:kpcfixed-model:disjoint-membership}.
The birth family covers all clauses of
\zcref{def:kpcfixed-model:first-output} and single birth
(\zcref{prop:kpcfixed-model:single-birth}). The continuation family covers the
same clauses at every consumed handle and
\zcref{thm:kpcfixed-invariants:unique-successor,thm:kpcfixed-invariants:key-isolation}.
The record family covers SOURCE, MERGE and their exhaustive recurrence
(\zcref{op:kpcfixed-operations:source,op:kpcfixed-operations:merge,def:kpcfixed-operations:classification})
with provenance and disjoint growth
(\zcref{thm:kpcfixed-invariants:source-provenance,thm:kpcfixed-invariants:set-membership-growth}).
The reading family covers reconstruction, incomplete and invalid evidence,
the named evidence assumptions, and path reading
(\zcref{sec:kpcfixed:interface,thm:kpcfixed-interface:membership-correctness,cor:kpcfixed-interface:path-reading}).
The acceptance family checks the separation in
\zcref{rem:kpcfixed-interface:record-evidence}.
Surplus controls vary valid other outputs without changing the record
union (\zcref{prop:kpcfixed-operations:merge-surplus}). Reading controls
include omitted merged branches and SOURCE-only attribution
(\zcref{rem:kpcfixed-interface:dependency-closure,op:kpcfixed-operations:source}).
Public extension controls use only public recipe and history
with independently supplied funding; contention observations establish no
inclusion or finality bound (\zcref{rem:kpcfixed:public-extension}).
The resource family measures a realization's costs without adding a
covenant condition or claiming that this specification supplies a cost
bound (\zcref{sec:kpcfixed:limitations,rem:kpcfixed:policy-floor}).
