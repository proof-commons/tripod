\section{Objects and the covenant}
\label{sec:kpcfixed:model}

\begin{definition}[History and outputs]
\label{def:kpcfixed-model:history}
A history $H$ is a finite, dependency-ordered sequence of valid events;
validity includes the covenant of every spent output. Each event $e$ has
finitely many inputs and an ordered output sequence $O(e)$. Positions start at zero.
Each output carries exactly one program, an asset field and a value field.
Each of the latter two fields is either explicit, publicly stating its
asset or amount, or in another form.
Output identities bind their creating event and position; event identities bind
immutable contents and dependencies. Outputs are fresh and can be consumed
at most once, by a later event. Every prefix of a history is itself a
history: dependencies and the validity of each retained event are unchanged.
Creating an output does not execute its covenant; a payment that cannot
later satisfy its spending condition may still be created.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Keyed membership]
\label{def:kpcfixed-model:keyed-membership}
Fix a public triple $q=(k,A,v)$ at covenant generation: a key, an asset
and a positive value that an explicit value field can carry under the
ordinary validity of the underlying history. Let $P_q$
be its authenticated program. Write $Q_q(x)$ when output $x$ carries
$P_q$, its asset field is explicit and states $A$, and its value field
is explicit and states $v$. A handle in $K_q$ is exactly an output satisfying $Q_q$ at the
first position in its creating event. No other field, including a nonce,
is part of this membership predicate.
A malformed payment is a first-position output bearing $P_q$ that fails $Q_q$.
Let $I_q(e)$ be the set of handles of $K_q$ that $e$ consumes.
An unconsumed handle is live, relative to the history or prefix being read.
\end{definition}

\begin{assumption}[Realization]
\label{assum:kpcfixed-model:realization}
A realization supplies immutable authenticated fields and distinct
programs for distinct triples, under its stated cryptographic assumptions.
It supplies a public recipe for $P_q$ and enforces exactly the spending
condition of \zcref{def:kpcfixed-model:first-output}, with no alternative
spending path. No secret can bypass it, including a private key or secret
data revealing the contents of a non-explicit field. The covenant can
authenticate the spent output's birth position, program, asset and value
fields and forms, and those fields of the first successor output.
No concrete mechanism or realization byte is presumed.
\end{assumption}

\begin{proposition}[Disjoint membership]
\label{prop:kpcfixed-model:disjoint-membership}
Under the distinct-program premise of \zcref{assum:kpcfixed-model:realization},
$K_q\cap K_p=\varnothing$ for distinct triples $q\ne p$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
An output in both classes would carry both $P_q$ and $P_p$ by
\zcref{def:kpcfixed-model:keyed-membership}. By \zcref{def:kpcfixed-model:history},
each output carries exactly one program, whereas
\zcref{assum:kpcfixed-model:realization} makes these programs
distinct. Thus no output belongs to both classes.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[The whole covenant]
\label{def:kpcfixed-model:first-output}
For output $x$ bearing $P_q$, let $b$ be its creating event and $e$ its
spending event. The exact spending condition is
\[
 x=O(b)_0\quad\land\quad Q_q(x)\quad\land\quad Q_q(O(e)_0).
\]
An absent first output makes the condition false. The three conjuncts are
called born-first, own-qualification and successor qualification, respectively.
Together they require qualified birth first and qualified continuation first. Each program,
asset and value comparison is against the constant supplied by $q$;
there is no comparison between inputs and outputs, no sum or resource
accounting and no authorization. There is no condition on other inputs
or outputs. Ordinary validity of the underlying history still applies.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Necessity of qualification]
\label{rem:kpcfixed-model:qualification-necessity}
Consider otherwise valid histories with adequate funding and admissible
alternative values, changing one ingredient at a time. Without born-first
in the spending condition, a
later output with $P_q$ and the exact explicit pair can be spent into a
qualified first output, contrary to nonmember unspendability
(\zcref{prop:kpcfixed-model:single-birth}). Without
own-qualification, a malformed payment with explicit asset $A$ and an
explicit value greater than $v$ can be spent into a qualified first output,
with the excess elsewhere: its spending event consumes no handle and is
a SOURCE, again contrary to \zcref{prop:kpcfixed-model:single-birth}. If successor
qualification checks only the program, a handle can be spent into a
first output with the wrong explicit value; that nonmember cannot be
spent under own-qualification. This contradicts the handle successor in
\zcref{thm:kpcfixed-invariants:unique-successor}, the no-handle case of
\zcref{def:kpcfixed-operations:classification} and the retained deposit in
\zcref{prop:kpcfixed-operations:merge-surplus}. Finally, if membership omits the pair while the
spending condition retains both-end qualification, a malformed payment
created without consuming a handle becomes a SOURCE handle whose own
qualification can never hold: its record is stranded, defeating public
extension as stated in \zcref{rem:kpcfixed:public-extension}. Thus weakening
the spending condition admits spends of nonmembers or continuation out of
the class, while weakening membership strands SOURCE records against public
extension. Membership and the spending condition must agree to retain both
nonmember unspendability and public extension.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[Single birth]
\label{prop:kpcfixed-model:single-birth}
Every event creates at most one handle of $K_q$. Every output bearing
$P_q$ that is not a handle is unspendable and has no record set.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Only position zero can belong to $K_q$, so there is at most one handle.
A nonmember bearing $P_q$ either has a later birth position or fails
$Q_q$ through its asset or value field or form. These immutable facts
falsify respectively the birth or own-qualification clause in every
attempted spend. Exact enforcement under
\zcref{assum:kpcfixed-model:realization} makes this refusal exhaustive.
The recurrence in \zcref{def:kpcfixed-operations:classification} assigns
sets only to handles; merely creating such a payment introduces no record.
\end{proof}
